# Carrier Contract Rates by Origin, Destination

Lists all-in container rates by origin and destination port.

## What's included

- Rates on carrier service contracts **in force on the chosen date** (no effective
  date, or effective on or before it, and no expiry, or expiring on or after it).
- Only contracts with a carrier among those chosen.
- Only rates from the origin ports and to the destination ports chosen.
- **Only rates with a freight class among those chosen.** A rate with no freight class
  doesn't appear.
- A rate set per several freight classes appears once for each.

## Things to know

- **All-in rate** is the base rate plus every fee on the rate and on the contract:
  fixed amounts, and percentages of the base rate. Per-booking and documentation
  fees aren't included.
- Ports are grouped by their port group, or else by country.
