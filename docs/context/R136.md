# Estimated Cargo Values

Estimates the value of upcoming sales by booking, for sales order lines and the
bookings they are assigned to.

## What's included

- **Sales orders only.**
- Lines on bookings sailing within the dates (the booking's planned departure, or
  else the shipment's). A line on no booking, or a booking with no sail date, is
  included when its order was placed within the dates, or has no order date.
- Narrowed by buyer. An order with no buyer appears only when the not-assigned choice
  is included.
- A line assigned to several bookings appears once for each.

## How the quantity is estimated

The first of these that applies:

1. The line is priced per the unit it is ordered in: the ordered quantity.
2. The line has an estimated quantity: that.
3. It is ordered in containers: containers × the average net weight of outbound
   cargo carrying the SKU, converted to the pricing unit.
4. Otherwise the average net weight of outbound cargo carrying the item.
5. Otherwise 0.
