# Sales by Commodity

Totals invoiced sales by customer, commodity and item, with tons and amounts.

## What's included

- **Invoices only,** dated within the dates, and only their lines.
- Only invoices whose buyer is among the customers chosen.
- An item with no commodity (or a line with no item) appears only when the
  not-assigned commodity choice is included.

## Things to know

- **The amount is worked out on the report:** price × the secondary quantity
  converted to the pricing unit, or price × quantity when there is no secondary
  quantity.
- Tons come from the secondary quantity.
- The processor comes from the fulfillment of the line's cargo.
