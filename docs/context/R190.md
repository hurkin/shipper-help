# Inventory by SKU, Work Zone

Shows what each SKU holds in each work zone as of a date.

## What's included

- Inventory rows with a **location**, dated on or before the end of the chosen day,
  totalled by SKU and the location's work zone.
- **A SKU completed by then is left out.**
- **A SKU and work zone whose quantities round to 0.0 are left out.**
- A SKU with no supplier, an item with no commodity, or a location with no work zone
  appears only when the not-assigned choice is included.
- SKU filter: an exact match. All location owners are included.
