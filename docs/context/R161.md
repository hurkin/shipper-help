# Booking Volume by Month, Destination

Totals booked containers by month and destination, for each shipper.

## What's included

- Bookings whose **booking** planned departure falls within the dates. A booking
  with no planned departure on the booking itself isn't counted.
- **Canceled bookings are left out.**
- **Only bookings with a shipper and a carrier among those chosen.** A booking with
  no shipper or no carrier never appears.
- Only bookings with a container quantity above 0.

## Things to know

- Quantities are **FEUs** (forty-foot equivalents): the booking's quantity × the TEUs
  of its container type (or else its container class), divided by 2. With neither,
  each container counts as one FEU.
- Ports are grouped by their port group, or else by country.
