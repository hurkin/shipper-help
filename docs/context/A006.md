# Sales Quotes

A sales quote offers product to a customer. If they accept, copy it to a sales
order or contract.

## Things to know

- You choose the customer. Your company is always the seller.
- The number comes from the sales quote number scheme, and a new quote starts in the
  sales quote starting queues.
- A quote with no lines doesn't show in the grid.
- Lines follow the same rules as sales order lines: Each or Container, Per from the
  item's selling unit class, and **Sales Amount** filled from the price list. See
  [Price Lists](I006.md).
- A quote has no contract picker. It isn't drawn from anything.
- **Is Firm Order** (on the General tab) is ticked on new quotes. Untick it for a
  quote that is still pending: it is then left out of searches for firm agreements.

## Fields

The agreement's and lines' fields work as on [sales orders](A004.md?id=fields).

## Auto Work Orders

The **Auto Work Orders** tab lists work orders Shipper creates on a cargo as soon
as one of this quote's lines is assigned or fulfilled on it. Every line takes
them unless it is turned off on the line's own **Auto Work Orders** tab, where a
line can also have its own. See [Work Orders](W001.md).

## Turning a quote into a sale

Use **Copy To > Sales Order** (or **Sales Contract**):

- The quote becomes the new agreement's parent.
- Prices are kept, because the customer accepted them. Copying a quote to a purchase
  sets prices to 0 instead.
- Additional terms are copied only to another quote.
- Auto Work Orders are copied to a sales order or contract, but not to a purchase,
  if you can create work orders.
- The new agreement is ordered now. It isn't put into any queue. Use its
  **Queues** button if it needs one.
- Save your changes first.

A quote can also be started from an inspection in ShipperXL Desktop, with the SKUs
the customer looked at.

## Completed, Posted and Delete

**Completed** starts any *Sales Quote Completed* scheduled jobs. Once **Posted**, the
quote is read-only, and only an owner can change the Posted date. **Delete** follows
the rules on [sales orders](A004.md?id=completed-posted-and-delete).

## Documentation

The **Documentation** tab lists the documentation tasks for this quote. It takes
its customer's, seller's and ship-to's tasks when it opens, or when you save a
change to them. Those can't be removed here. Add any others the quote needs. Its
bookings take these tasks. Save to keep changes. See [Booking Documents](D001.md).
