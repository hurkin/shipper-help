# Inventory Value by Item

Values inventory on hand as of a date at average purchase cost, totalled by
commodity, item and work zone.

## What's included

- Inventory rows with a **location**, dated on or before the end of the chosen day.
- **A SKU completed by then is left out.**
- **A SKU and location whose quantities round to 0.0 are left out** before totalling.
- An item with no commodity, or a location with no work zone, appears only when the
  not-assigned choice is included.
- All suppliers and locations are included.

## How it is valued

The same as [Inventory Value](R160.md): average purchase price over the SKU's
purchase lines, applied to quantity (count units) or weight, plus average variable
costs. A SKU with no purchase lines has no value.
