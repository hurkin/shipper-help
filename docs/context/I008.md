# Inspections

An inspection records who inspected which SKUs or containers, when, and what they
found, such as a customer visit or an inbound quality check. Inspections are
entered in ShipperXL Desktop.

## Things to know

- **The inspection type decides the fields.** Each type has its own custom fields,
  some of which may be required. See [Inspection Types](I007.md).
- **Targets** are the SKUs and containers inspected. Add SKUs one at a time or
  several at once, and add containers by cargo number.
- **Create Quote** starts a sales quote for the SKUs you tick, for a customer who
  wants to buy what they saw.
- **Clone** starts a new inspection from this one.
- The person who created or last edited the inspection is printed on the inspection
  form.
- Attachments, such as photos, can be added.
