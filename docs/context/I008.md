# Inspections

An inspection records who inspected which SKUs or cargo, when, and what they
found, such as a customer visit or an inbound quality check. Inspections are under
**Lists > Inspections**. Inspections recorded in the mobile app's
[Inbound Inspection](M08.md) show here too.

## The list

- There is a row for each SKU or cargo an inspection looked at, so an inspection of
  three SKUs is three rows. One with nothing on it is a single row.
- It starts with the last 7 days, newest first. Clear **Inspected To** to leave the
  end open, or clear both dates to see every inspection.
- Narrow it by **Type**, **Inspected By** and **SKU**. The search finds the start of
  a SKU or cargo number, or part of the inspector, type or comment.

## Things to know

- **The inspection type decides the fields.** Each type has its own fields, some of
  which may be required. See [Inspection Types](I007.md). The type can't be changed
  once the inspection is saved.
- **Inspected By** and the type are required. A new inspection is inspected by you,
  if you are one of your company's contacts. The inspection date starts at now.
- **Related Items** are the SKUs and cargo inspected. Add a SKU or a cargo at a
  time, or **Add Multiple SKUs…** to tick several from a search. Double-click a
  row's comment to change it. Changes are kept when you save.
- **Directions** opens directions to every SKU on the inspection: to the
  SKU's default location (its coordinates, or else its address), or else to the
  SKU's own map position. SKUs with none of these are left out, and Shipper names
  them. Cargo is not included. Save your changes first.
- **Create Quote** starts a sales quote to the inspector, at their first address,
  with a line for each SKU you tick in **Related Items**. Lines start at a quantity
  and price of 0. It needs rights to sales quotes.
- **Clone** starts a new inspection with the same type, inspector, related items
  and details, dated now. Attachments aren't copied.
- **Reports** prints the inspection form.
- Attachments, such as photos, can be added once the inspection is saved.
- **Delete** removes the inspection with its details, related items and
  attachments.

## On cargo and SKUs

[Cargo](B003.md) and [SKUs](I004.md) have an **Inspections** tab listing the
inspections that include them. **Add Inspection** starts a new one with that cargo
or SKU already on it.
