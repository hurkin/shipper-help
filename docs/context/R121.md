# Shipment Advice

Tells a customer about their upcoming shipments: each sales line, the booking it is
on, the vessel, ports and dates. Changes in the last few days can be highlighted.

## What's included

- **Sales order and contract lines that are assigned to a booking.** A line not on
  any booking never appears.
- The booking's planned arrival must be within the dates.
- The line's agreement must have the customer (or a party related to the customer)
  as its **buyer or ship-to**.
- **The booking's shipper must be the agreement's seller,** or the customer or a
  related party. Otherwise the line is left out.
- An item with no commodity appears only when the not-assigned choice is included.
- **Only changed rows:** limits it to lines whose booking, shipment, booking
  quantity or assignment changed within the highlight period.

## Things to know

- **Prices show only when the customer is the buyer** (or related to the buyer).
  A ship-to party sees no prices.
- The processor's name shows only when the customer is set to have processors
  disclosed. Otherwise the work zone is shown.
- The letter of credit shown is the one linked to the agreement, or else to the
  booking and agreement together.
- Containers are counted once they have been delivered to the carrier.

## Sending it from a scheduled job

A scheduled job sends the report even when it would be empty. To skip customers
with nothing to report, give the job an **Iteration SQL** that returns no rows when
there's nothing to send. For example, for the next 15 days back to 12 months ahead:

```sql
DECLARE @StartArrival DATETIME = CAST(DATEADD(d, -15, GETUTCDATE()) AS date);
DECLARE @FinishArrival DATETIME = CAST(DATEADD(m, 12, GETUTCDATE()) AS date);
WITH PartyList AS (
  SELECT PartyId FROM Party WHERE PartyId = @PartyId
  UNION SELECT FromPartyId FROM PartyRelation WHERE ToPartyId = @PartyId
  UNION SELECT ToPartyId FROM PartyRelation WHERE FromPartyId = @PartyId
)
SELECT COUNT(*)
FROM dbo.Agreement A
JOIN dbo.AgreementType AT ON AT.AgreementTypeId = A.AgreementTypeId
  AND AT.Mnemonic IN ('SORDER','SCONTRACT')
JOIN dbo.AgreementItem AI ON AI.AgreementId = A.AgreementId
JOIN dbo.AgreementItemAssignment AIA ON AIA.AgreementItemId = AI.AgreementItemId
JOIN dbo.Shipment S ON S.ShipmentId = AIA.ShipmentId
JOIN dbo.Booking B ON B.ShipmentId = S.ShipmentId
WHERE B.PlannedArrival BETWEEN @StartArrival AND @FinishArrival + 0.99999
  AND (A.Buyer_PartyId IN (SELECT PartyId FROM PartyList)
    OR A.ShipTo_PartyId IN (SELECT PartyId FROM PartyList))
  AND (S.Shipper_PartyId = A.Seller_PartyId
    OR S.Shipper_PartyId IN (SELECT PartyId FROM PartyList))
HAVING COUNT(*) > 0
```
