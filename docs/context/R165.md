# Scale Ticket Summary

A complete list of scale tickets for each scale, for audits of every weight taken.

## What's included

- **Tickets with both gross and tare weighed,** where either weight was taken within
  the dates.
- Only tickets from the scales chosen. A scale with no work zone appears only when
  the not-assigned choice is included.
- Run for a company: tickets where the company is the buyer or seller of the
  agreement fulfilled, the work order's worker or contact, or the cargo's processor
  or owner.

## Things to know

- **Delta hours** is the time between the tare and gross weights.
- Quantity is the work order's inventory, else the cargo's inventory, else the
  fulfilled quantity, else the work order quantity.
- **A starred ticket's load fulfilled more than one agreement line.**
