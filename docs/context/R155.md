# SKUs Available to Sell by Item

The same as [SKUs Available to Sell](R127.md), grouped by item.

## What's included

- **Active SKUs that aren't completed,** of product items only.
- A SKU with no supplier, or an item with no commodity, appears only when the
  not-assigned choice is included.
- **Only SKUs with something available:** available to sell, or, with the other
  option, still at the supplier plus on hand.
- Figures are shown in the unit you choose. A SKU whose figures aren't in that unit
  doesn't appear.
- Keywords search the item description, or match an item number exactly.
- You can require a positive or negative result on a field of an inspection type.
- Run from an inspection, it lists that inspection's SKUs.

## Things to know

- Hay analysis figures come from the SKU's latest public *Hay Analysis* inspection.
- The quote column marks SKUs on open sales quotes.
