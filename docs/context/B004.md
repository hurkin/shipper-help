# Scale Tickets

A scale ticket is the weight record for a load: gross, tare and net, who drove,
and the scale's pictures. Most tickets belong to a cargo.

## Things to know

- **Opening a ticket from the list** takes you to its cargo's Scale Ticket tab when
  it has one, or to the ticket on its own otherwise.
- **The ticket number comes from the scale.** Each scale has its own number format
  and counter, set in [Scales](B007.md).
- **Adding a ticket to a cargo** links it both ways. A ticket linked to a work order
  on a cargo is linked to the cargo too.
- **Net weight is gross minus tare.** On an inbound cargo, the net weight sets the
  unit weight of everything received on it. Changing the weights later recalculates
  that inventory.
- **Drayman** is the trucking company. **Operator** and **Vehicle** list that
  drayman's people and vehicles first.

## Reading the scale

When the scale has a kiosk, **Start Reading** shows its live weight and picture.

- The kiosk sends a reading about every 30 seconds while something is on the scale.
  A reading older than about 90 seconds is marked out of date, and **Capture** is
  unavailable.
- **Capture** fills the gross first, then the tare. If the second weight is heavier,
  Shipper swaps them, because the truck was weighed empty first. The weighed time is
  stamped when you capture.
- The camera picture is attached when you save. On a cargo's **Scale Ticket** tab,
  the cargo's Save saves the ticket.
- **Print Tags** sends the cargo's tags to the kiosk's label printer, so it needs a
  cargo.
