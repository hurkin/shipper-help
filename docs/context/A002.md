# Purchase Orders

A purchase order is a one-time purchase. It is common for the buy side of a
back-to-back trade. For product bought from a grower over time, use a
[purchase contract](A003.md).

## Creating one

- You choose the seller (the supplier). Your company is always the buyer, and you
  are its purchase agent if your sign-in email is one of the company's contacts.
- The number comes from the purchase order number scheme. The order starts in the
  purchase order starting queues, with your default terms templates in
  **Additional Terms**.
- An order with no lines doesn't show in the grid.
- The seller's address and contact lists show only that seller's. Changing the
  seller clears them.

## Lines

- **Unit Type** is Each or Container. **Container Type** appears only for
  Container.
- **Per** is limited to units of the item's purchased unit class. If the item is
  bought only by its purchased unit, Per is fixed to it.
- **Purchase Amount** fills from the cost list when you change the item, Per, SKU or
  container type, using the seller and the order date. Opening a saved line never
  changes it. See [Cost Lists](I005.md).
- **Inventory SKU** is a real lot you will hold. **Customer SKU** (a non-inventory
  SKU) and **Additional SKU Detail** are free text that print with the line.
- If the header names a purchase contract, each line can point at one of the
  contract's lines. Fulfillment then counts on the contract too.
- **Variable Costs** list costs you expect to pay later (dray, tarping, insurance),
  so reports can show an all-in cost. The **Auto Bill** box is used only by
  scheduled jobs set up for you.
- **Assignments** put a line on a booking. Each needs a quantity, a booking and a
  work zone.

## Fields

The agreement's and lines' fields work as on [sales orders](A004.md?id=fields).

## Auto Work Orders

The **Auto Work Orders** tab lists work orders Shipper creates on a cargo as soon
as one of this order's lines is assigned or fulfilled on it. Every line takes
them unless it is turned off on the line's own **Auto Work Orders** tab, where a
line can also have its own. See [Work Orders](W001.md).

## How it is fulfilled

An inbound cargo fulfills the line, usually in units such as bales. For a
back-to-back trade, the line can be fulfilled by the outbound container with no
inventory at all. See [Inventory Basics](../instructions/inventory-basics.md).

**Create Bill** builds and saves a bill from this order, then opens it. It needs
write access to bills, and the order must be saved. If the order already has a
bill, Shipper names it and asks before creating another. Fulfilled purchase lines
can also be billed in bulk with [Create Bills](W005.md).

## Completed and Posted

- **Completed** starts any *Purchase Order Completed* scheduled jobs. With
  accounting integration, completed orders are picked up and posted.
- Once **Posted**, the order and its lines are read-only. Only an owner can change
  the Posted date.
