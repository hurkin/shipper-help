# Work Order Schedule

Lists planned work orders in the scheduler's order, with their bookings, SKUs and
locations, to share the plan.

## What's included

- Work orders **planned to start** within the dates (whole days in your time zone).
- You can leave out finished work orders.
- A work order with no work zone, work center or worker appears only when the
  not-assigned choice is included for that list.
- Only work orders with a work item type.
- **A work order can appear more than once:** once per order line assigned to it
  (or to its cargo), and once per tag waiting on it.

## Things to know

- **Order follows the scheduler.** Work orders are sorted by the UTC day of their
  planned start, then by the scheduler's order. A work order planned late in the
  evening can fall on the next day. See [Work Order Scheduler](W004.md).
- The SKU shown is the tag's SKU when tags are waiting, else the order line's.
- The tarping party comes from the *TARPPARTY* custom field on the order line, or
  else on the SKU.
