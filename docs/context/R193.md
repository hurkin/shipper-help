# Work In Progress Inventory

Shows inventory that was in process at a point in time: taken out onto a cargo or
work order that wasn't finished yet.

## What's included

- **On cargo:** inventory taken out by a cargo (directly or through its work order)
  that was loaded but not yet delivered to the carrier at the chosen time.
- **On work orders:** inventory on a work order that had started but not finished at
  that time.
- **Purchased product in transit:** purchase lines fulfilled by a cargo loaded but not
  yet delivered to the carrier.
- Only rows dated on or before the chosen time.
- Narrowed by work zone. Rows with no work zone appear only when the not-assigned
  choice is included.

## Things to know

- Quantities are shown as positive: what is out of stock and in process.
- The location and work zone are the cargo's location when it has one, else the
  inventory's, else the SKU's default work zone.
