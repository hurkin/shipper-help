# Inventory Value

Values inventory on hand as of a date at average purchase cost, by location and SKU,
with variable costs added.

## What's included

- Inventory rows with a **location**, dated on or before the end of the chosen day.
- **A SKU completed by then is left out.**
- **A SKU and location whose quantities round to 0.0 are left out.**
- A SKU with no supplier, an item with no commodity, or a location with no work zone
  appears only when the not-assigned choice is included.
- Location filter: the short code, or any short code starting with it. SKU filter:
  an exact match.

## How it is valued

- **Price** is the average purchase price of the SKU over all its purchase order and
  contract lines.
- When those lines are priced per a count unit (and the SKU is counted in one too),
  value = quantity × average price. Otherwise value = average price × the inventory's
  weight, converted to the pricing unit.
- **A SKU bought per two different units** (for example per ton on one contract and
  per bale on another) **appears once for each.**
- **Additional cost** is the average of the purchase lines' variable costs per unit,
  times the raw quantity plus the processed quantity converted to raw units. It is
  listed only for SKUs and locations with a positive raw balance.
- A SKU with no purchase lines has no value.
