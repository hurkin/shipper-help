# Sales Orders

A sales order is a single sale to a customer. Its lines say what is sold, and
cargo and work orders fulfill them.

## Creating one

- You choose the customer. Your company is always the seller, and you are its sales
  agent if your sign-in email is one of the company's contacts.
- The number comes from the sales order number scheme. The order starts in the
  sales order starting queues, with your default terms templates already in
  **Additional Terms**.
- An order with no lines doesn't show in the grid. Add a line before you leave it.
- Saving keeps whatever is entered. Required fields are marked but don't stop a
  save.

## Lines

- **Unit Type** is Each or Container. **Container Type** appears only for
  Container.
- **Per** is limited to units of the item's selling unit class. If the item is sold
  only by its default unit, Per is fixed to it.
- **Estimated** appears only when Per differs from Unit Type, such as containers
  sold per ton. It holds the expected tons.
- **Sales Amount** fills from the price list when you change the item, Per, SKU or
  container type. Opening a saved line never changes it. See
  [Price Lists](I006.md) for how a price is chosen.
- If the header names a sales contract, each line can point at one of the
  contract's lines. Fulfillment on the order then counts on the contract too.
- **Assignments** put a line on a booking. Each needs a quantity, a booking and a
  work zone. The quantity starts at what is left to assign.
- A line's **Fulfilment** tab is read-only. Quantities are entered on the cargo or
  work order.

## Copy To

Copy To makes a new agreement of any type you can create, with this order's
lines and line notes.

- The new record is ordered now. Completed, posted, canceled and similar dates
  aren't copied.
- Prices are kept only for the same type, or when a quote becomes a sale.
  Otherwise they are 0.
- Additional terms are copied only to the same type.
- Copying a sale to a purchase asks for the seller. Your company keeps its side.
- Custom field values aren't copied, and the copy isn't put into any queue.
- Save your changes first. If a line fails to copy, you still land on the new
  record and are told what failed.

## Create Invoice

**Create Invoice** builds and saves an invoice from this order's fulfilled lines,
then opens it. It needs write access to invoices, and the order must be saved. If
the order already has an invoice, Shipper names it and asks before creating
another. See [Create Invoices](../instructions/create-invoices.md).

## Completed and Posted

- **Completed** says the order is done. It starts any *Sales Order Completed*
  scheduled jobs. With accounting integration, completed orders are picked up and
  posted.
- Once **Posted**, the order and its lines are read-only. Only an owner can change
  the Posted date, and then nothing else.

## Letter of Credit

When the customer pays by letter of credit, choose it under **Letter of Credit** on
the **Terms** tab and save. The order has one letter of credit: choosing another
replaces it, and clearing the field removes it. The order then takes the letter of
credit's documentation tasks, and it shows on the **Related** tab. You need
permission to see letters of credit. See [Letters of Credit](IV07.md).

## Documentation

The **Documentation** tab lists the documentation tasks for this order. It takes
its customer's, seller's and ship-to's tasks when it opens, or when you save a
change to them. Those can't be removed here. Add any others the order needs. Its
bookings take these tasks. Save to keep changes. See [Booking Documents](D001.md).
