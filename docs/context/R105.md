# Work Order Statement

Lists finished work orders with their quantities, rates and amounts, for paying or
checking workers.

## What's included

- Work orders **finished** in the dates you choose (whole days in your time zone).
  Run for a booking, it lists every work order on the booking, on its cargo or on
  the booking itself, finished or not.
- Narrowed by work zone, worker and work item type. A work order with no work zone
  or no worker appears only when the not-assigned choice is included.
- **Reference filter:** matches Reference 1 or Reference 2 exactly. *BILL* doesn't
  find *BILLY*.
- **Billed:** choose unbilled work orders, billed ones, or both.

## Things to know

- When you change the dates, check the workers and items again. They may not all
  be selected by default.
- The cargo number shows the booking's destination country code after it, when the
  booking has a destination port.
