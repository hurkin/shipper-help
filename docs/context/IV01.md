# Invoices

An invoice is what a customer owes you. Your company is always the seller and the
customer the buyer.

## Creating one

- **From a sales order or booking** (recommended): Shipper builds the lines from what
  was fulfilled. See [Create Invoices](../instructions/create-invoices.md).
- **New, by hand:** you choose the customer. The number comes from the invoice
  number scheme, the date is today, and the invoice starts in the invoice starting
  queues.
- **Terms** for a new invoice are, in order: terms whose short code is *INVOICE*, the
  customer's terms, your company's terms, or else the active terms with the fewest
  days due. Terms can't be changed on the invoice.
- **Due** is the invoice date plus the terms' days. Changing the invoice date moves
  it.

## Things to know

- **Address blocks** are the addresses printed on the invoice. Picking a party or
  address rewrites its block, and you can edit the block afterwards. A party with no
  address prints just its name.
- **Lines** print their own item code and description. Picking an item copies its
  number and description, and you can change them. **Per** follows the item's
  selling units. An empty amount saves as 0.
- **Pro Forma** marks a pro forma invoice for reports. It doesn't stop a completed
  invoice from going to accounting.
- The **Related** tab lists every agreement tied to the invoice.

## Grid filters

- **Open:** not fully paid, including new invoices with no lines.
- **Completed:** completed within the dates.
- **Paid:** fully paid, by the date of the last payment.

An invoice can be in more than one view, such as completed but unpaid.

## Payments

- Anyone who can edit invoices can add or change a payment, even on a posted
  invoice, and set its Posted date.
- Once a payment is posted, only an owner can change, unpost or delete it.
- Posting a payment starts any *Invoice Payment Posted* scheduled jobs.
- With accounting integration, payments recorded in accounting come back to
  Shipper, and an invoice paid in full is marked completed.

## Completed, Posted and Delete

- **Completed** starts any *Invoice Completed* scheduled jobs. With accounting
  integration, completed invoices are sent to accounting and posted. One dated in
  the future waits until its date.
- Once **Posted**, the invoice and its lines are read-only. Only an owner can change
  the Posted date.
- An invoice can be deleted only if it isn't posted and has no payments, posted
  lines, generated documents or inventory on its lines. It leaves its queues.
