# Inventory Cargo Activity

Shows inventory moved by cargo and work orders, with the cargo's weight, contents
and fulfillment side by side. Useful for finding inconsistencies in data entry.

## What's included

- Run for a cargo, a booking (its cargo and work orders), an agreement (cargo and
  work orders that fulfill it), a SKU, or a period.
- For a SKU or a period: only rows on a cargo or a work order, dated within the
  dates. Moves and adjustments aren't included.
- **Only rows with a location.**
- Narrowed by work zone. A location with no work zone appears only when the
  not-assigned choice is included.

## Things to know

- Average weights divide the cargo's net weight by its raw units, processed units,
  contents quantity and fulfilled quantity. When these disagree, something was
  entered wrong.
- The date shown is the cargo's load (or unload) time, or else the work order's
  finish (or start).
