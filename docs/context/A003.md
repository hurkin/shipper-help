# Purchase Contracts

A purchase contract is product bought over a period of time, usually from a grower
or supplier for a set of SKUs. Each inbound load fulfills a line.

## Things to know

- You choose the seller. Your company starts as the buyer, and you are its purchase
  agent if your sign-in email is one of the company's contacts.
- The number comes from the purchase contract number scheme. A new contract starts
  in the purchase contract starting queues, with your default terms templates in
  **Additional Terms**.
- A contract with no lines doesn't show in the grid.
- **Fulfilled** includes everything fulfilled on purchase orders drawn from the
  contract. **Ordered** counts only the contract's own lines.
- A contract fulfilled through its orders is on the grid's **Fulfilled** tab, dated
  by the latest fulfillment on it or its orders. Every agreement is on exactly one
  of **Not Fulfilled** and **Fulfilled**.
- Put one SKU on each line. When an inbound cargo looks up a SKU, it finds the
  purchase line that holds it.

## Lines

- **Unit Type** is Each or Container. Bales are usually Each.
- **Per** is limited to units of the item's purchased unit class, or fixed when the
  item is bought only by its purchased unit.
- **Purchase Amount** fills from the cost list when you change the item, Per, SKU or
  container type. See [Cost Lists](I005.md).
- **Estimated** appears only when Per differs from Unit Type, for example bales
  bought per ton. It holds the expected tons.
- **Variable Costs** list costs you expect to pay later, for an all-in cost, with
  the cost per unit and total estimated cost beneath. See
  [Variable Costs](A004.md?id=variable-costs).

## Fields

The agreement's and lines' fields work as on [sales orders](A004.md?id=fields).

## Auto Work Orders

The **Auto Work Orders** tab lists work orders Shipper creates on a cargo as soon
as one of this contract's lines is assigned or fulfilled on it. Every line takes
them unless it is turned off on the line's own **Auto Work Orders** tab, where a
line can also have its own. See [Work Orders](W001.md).

## Billing the grower

- [Create Bills](W005.md) with **Fulfillment** makes supplier bills from what was
  fulfilled over a date range, priced from the contract lines.
- Deposits paid outside Shipper can be recorded against the contract. See
  [Grower Deposits](../instructions/grower-deposits.md).

## Completed, Posted and Delete

- **Completed** starts any *Purchase Contract Completed* scheduled jobs. With
  accounting integration, completed contracts are picked up and posted.
- Once **Posted**, the contract is read-only. Only an owner can change the Posted
  date.
- **Delete** follows the rules on [sales orders](A004.md?id=completed-posted-and-delete):
  not once posted, nor while any line has fulfillments.
