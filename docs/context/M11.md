# My Work Orders

My Work Orders lists work orders in the mobile app, so crews can see what to do
next and record it.

## Things to know

- The list can show work orders not yet finished, or those finished in the last
  7 days.
- A work order shows its cargo, its lines and the inventory recorded against it.
- Inventory is recorded with the movement screens, such as [Receive](M01.md),
  [Process](M04.md) and [Ship](M05.md). Each needs its own feature.
- Recording inventory starts a work order that hadn't started. Process and Ship can
  also mark it finished.
- Work orders are planned and edited in [Work Orders](W001.md).
