# Physical Inventory

Prints a physical count worksheet with estimated weights, including product that was
in process at the time of the count.

## What's included

- **The counts stamped with the chosen Checked time,** by location and SKU.
- Narrowed by work zone, location owner and supplier. A location with no work zone
  or owner, or a SKU with no supplier, appears only when the not-assigned choice is
  included.
- **Work in process (WIP)** is added beside the counts: inventory taken out by a
  cargo that was loaded but not yet delivered to the carrier at the Checked time,
  and inventory on a work order that is on a cargo. WIP counts at the cargo's own
  location when it has one.
- WIP for a SKU and location that has **never** been counted there gets its own
  row with a count of 0.

## Things to know

- Estimated weight is (count + WIP) × the SKU's average unit weight, for raw and
  processed. The processed unit weight is worked out from the work orders that
  turned raw into processed up to the Checked time.
