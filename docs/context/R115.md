# Commercial Invoice

Prints an invoice as a commercial invoice, with the booking's vessel, ports and
dates and the agreements it relates to.

## What's included

- Run for one invoice. **Only invoices with lines print.**
- Booking details come from the bookings linked to the invoice. **An invoice linked
  to more than one booking prints its lines once for each.**
- **Related agreements** are the sales orders, contracts and quotes fulfilled on the
  linked bookings or their cargo, plus the letter of credit the invoice points to.

## Things to know

- **The amount is worked out on the report.** When a line has a secondary quantity,
  it is converted to the line's pricing unit and multiplied by the price.
  Otherwise the quantity is used. A secondary quantity with no unit is treated as
  pounds.
- US ports print with their state.
- Total short and metric tons are the linked booking's.
