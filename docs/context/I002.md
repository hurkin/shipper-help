# Commodities

A commodity is the general product, such as alfalfa or timothy. Items are the
specific products made from it.

## Things to know

- For plant products, give each commodity its own **Scientific Name**.
- Keep commodities general. Rather than "Fescue Hay" and "Fescue Straw", make
  *Fescue* the commodity and the hay and straw separate items.
- Inventory balances can be filtered by commodity.
- Commodities are deactivated, not deleted.
