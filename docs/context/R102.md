# Scale Weight Ticket

Prints the weight ticket for a scale ticket, a cargo, or every cargo on a booking.

## What's included

- Run from a scale ticket or cargo, it prints that ticket. Run from a booking, it
  prints the ticket of every cargo on the booking.
- **Run for a company and a day,** it prints tickets for cargo that:
  - fulfill an agreement where the company is the buyer or seller, or, with no
    agreement, carry inventory of a SKU the company supplies;
  - have **both gross and tare weighed**;
  - were weighed that day, or had their container details, contents or fulfillment
    changed that day; and
  - have inventory or a fulfilled quantity that doesn't net to zero.
- **A ticket prints once for each attachment** (camera picture) it has.

## Things to know

- Net weight is gross minus tare. The kilogram net is converted from the scale's
  unit.
- **Verified gross** is the net weight plus the container's own tare, taken from
  tare pounds, or else tare kilograms. It is blank when the container has no tare.
- The extra line lists the purchase SKUs fulfilled by the cargo (quantity, unit,
  SKU and supplier) and its contents.
- Without a cargo, the work order's Reference 1 is shown as the cargo number.
