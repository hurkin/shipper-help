# Agreement Statement

A statement of account for agreements: the bills or invoices raised against each,
and the payments made, up to a date.

## What's included

- Run for one agreement, for a bill (every agreement tied to it), or for a company.
- **For a company, choose a date or the report is blank.** It lists agreements of
  every type where the company is the buyer, seller or ship-to and the date falls
  between the agreement's effective date (or the day before it was ordered) and its
  expiry (or a year after it was ordered).
- Bills and invoices tied to the agreement, by a link or by a line drawn from one of
  its lines, **from the agreement's own seller**, dated on or before the day after
  the chosen date.
- Payments on those bills, dated on or before the day after the chosen date.

## Things to know

- **Bill amounts are shown only for bills linked to the agreement.** A bill tied only
  through its lines appears without an amount.
- The amount is worked out on the report: price × the secondary quantity converted to
  the pricing unit, or price × quantity.
- Payments may be wrong if deposits to suppliers aren't recorded properly. Record a
  deposit as a bill in Shipper, let it go to accounting, and pay it there.
  See [Grower Deposits](../instructions/grower-deposits.md).
