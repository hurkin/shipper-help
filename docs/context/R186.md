# Certificate of Origin (Korea)

Prints the Korea free trade certificate of origin for a booking. It is a document:
create it on the booking first, then print it.

## What's included

- Run for one document. **The document must be on a booking.**
- **The booking must have a sales order or contract line assigned to it or fulfilled
  on it,** or the report is blank.
- The exporter, producer, importer, serial number, goods, quantity, HS number,
  preference criterion and country come from the document's fields with those names
  (*EXPORTER*, *PRODUCER*, *IMPORTER*, *SERIAL*, *GOODS*, *QUANTITY*, *HSNO*,
  *PREFERENCE*, *COUNTRY*).

## Things to know

- Phone, fax and email come from the parties: the exporter is the booking's shipper,
  the producer the sales agreement's seller, and the importer its buyer.
- **Goods** prints one row per line of the field. Write a line as *HS number:
  description* to put the HS number in its own column.
- The person printing is named on the certificate.
