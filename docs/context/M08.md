# Inbound Inspection

Inbound Inspection records a quality check on product as it arrives, from the
mobile app.

## What you can scan

- **An inventory tag:** the inspection is tied to the cargo the tag was first
  received on.
- **A work order:** the inspection is tied to the work order's cargo.
- **A SKU tag or SKU:** the inspection is tied to that SKU.

If the scan matches none of these, the inspection still saves, tied to nothing.
Check the barcode before you save.

## Things to know

- **The inspection type decides the fields,** such as moisture readings or quality
  checkboxes. See [Inspection Types](I007.md).
- The inspection is dated when it is saved, with the inspector you choose.
- **Decimal readings are rounded.** A reading of 12.5 shows as typed, but the
  number Shipper keeps for it is 13.
- **Field values are cut off at 100 characters.** Put longer notes in the comment.
- Saved inspections show in [Inspections](I008.md), where they can be edited and
  given attachments.
