# Agreement Summary

Summarizes a company's agreements as of a date: each line's ordered, fulfilled and
remaining quantities and weights, its value, and payments made.

## What's included

- Run for one agreement, or for a company and an "as of" date.
- **For a company,** agreements where the company is the **buyer or seller** (not
  just the ship-to) that:
  - became effective within the year before the date (or, with no effective date,
    were ordered within that year); and
  - expire on or after the date (or, with no expiry date, were ordered before it).
- Narrowed by agreement type.

## How the figures work

- **Only fulfillment on or before the date counts.** Its date is the cargo's unload
  (or load) time, else the work order's finish, else the scale ticket's weigh time,
  else when the fulfillment was entered.
- **Fulfilled** includes fulfillment on child lines (orders drawn from a contract
  line).
- **Balance** is ordered minus fulfilled, never below 0.
- **Weight** is the net weight of the cargo that fulfilled the line and its child
  lines, in the line's pricing unit.
- **Fulfilled 2 and Balance 2:** when the line is priced per a weight unit, the
  actual weight against the estimated quantity. Otherwise the fulfilled quantity
  converted to the pricing unit.
- **Fulfilled value** is weight × price.
- **Payments** are on bills and invoices linked to the agreement, from the
  agreement's seller, paid on or before the date. Child agreements' payments aren't
  included.
