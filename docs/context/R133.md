# Physical Inventory Values

Values a physical count: counted quantities and work in process, priced at average
purchase cost, with work order and variable costs allocated, compared to what
Shipper had on hand.

## What's included

- **Counts stamped with the chosen Checked time** that aren't zero, grouped by SKU,
  location owner and work zone.
- **Work in process,** marked **+**: inventory on cargo that was loaded but not yet
  delivered to the carrier at the Checked time, or on a work order that had started
  but not finished.
- **Purchased product on cargo in transit,** marked **^**: purchase lines fulfilled by
  cargo loaded but not yet delivered to the carrier.
- Narrowed by work zone, location owner and supplier. Rows with none appear only
  when the not-assigned choice is included.

## Things to know

- **Current** quantity is what Shipper had on hand at the Checked time, and **Delta**
  is count minus current. Current shows only on counted rows, or when a SKU has
  just one row.
- Purchase value uses the SKU's average purchase price across all its purchase
  lines, converted from its average unit weight.
- **Work order costs** are finished work orders with a rate, on cargo that brought in
  the SKU, up to the Checked time. They are allocated by the share of the SKU's
  total received units that was counted (never more than all of it).
- **Variable costs** are the average of the purchase lines' variable costs per unit.
- If the report times out, include *Unassigned* in the work zone choices.
