# Beneficiary Certificate

Prints the beneficiary certificate a letter of credit requires for a booking. It is a
document: create it on the booking first, then print it.

## What's included

- Run for one document. **The document must be on a booking.**
- **The booking must be linked to a letter of credit,** or the report is blank.
- The declaration lines come from the document's fields *DECL1* to *DECL4*.
- The agreement numbers and references are the sales orders and contracts fulfilled
  on the booking or its cargo.
- Buyer and seller addresses are the letter of credit's.

## Things to know

- The letter of credit number is printed unless a beneficiary certificate
  documentation task on the booking says not to.
- The issue date is the booking's planned departure.
