# Terms Templates

Terms templates are ready-made paragraphs for an agreement's **Additional Terms**,
kept per agreement type.

## Things to know

- **Default** templates are added automatically to every new agreement of that type,
  in sort order.
- **Sort Order** sets the order templates are listed and added in.
- On an agreement, **Add Terms** lists the type's templates (defaults first) and
  appends the ones you tick, in list order, after a blank line. So if you cleared the
  defaults, you can put them back.
- Clone carries Additional Terms over only when the new agreement is the same
  type.
- Templates are deleted, not deactivated. Deleting needs delete rights.
