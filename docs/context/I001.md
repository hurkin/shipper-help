# Items

An item is something you buy, sell or do. It is either a **product** (raw or
processed, held in inventory through SKUs) or a **service** (trucking, pressing,
commission, used on work orders). Define items carefully: they appear on every
agreement, bill and work order.

## Things to know

- **Product or service is fixed** once the item is saved.
- **Item Number** must be unique. It can be changed later, but an item used on an
  agreement, bill, SKU or another item's bill of materials can't be deleted. Mark
  it inactive instead.
- **Short Description** shows in lists, invoices and certificates. **Description**
  shows on work orders and other documents that need the full wording.
- **Selling and purchasing units:**
  - The sold unit class and purchased unit class limit which units agreement lines,
    bills and work orders can price per.
  - **Sold only by this unit type** (or **Purchased only by this unit type**) fixes
    Per to that unit everywhere.
  - **Default Price** and **Default Cost** can be entered only with that box ticked.
    They are used when no price or cost list matches and the line is per that unit.
- **Both unit classes lock once the item has SKUs.**
- **Raw to Processed Ratio** relates raw units to processed units for the product.
- For a service, **Default Movement Type** and **Require a worker for work orders**
  are used by ShipperXL Desktop: choosing the service on a work order sets its
  movement type, and a cargo won't save with a work order for it that has no
  worker.
- **Accounting:** the external item number, class and accounts are copied to bill
  and invoice lines when the line is saved. Bills take the expense account and
  invoices the income account. Changing them here doesn't change lines already
  saved.
- **Translations** give the descriptions in other languages, for users working in
  those languages, and regional spellings, such as *green color* (en-US) and *green
  colour* (en-CA).
- **Barcode** holds a UPC, PLU, ISBN or similar code.
- **Accounting overrides** let the item's external codes differ by work zone, work
  center, the seller's country or region. They are set in ShipperXL Desktop. See
  [Accounting Integration](../instructions/accounting-integration.md?id=how-accounting-codes-are-chosen).
- In ShipperXL Desktop, **Container Types** limit which container types the item
  can ship in (none ticked allows all), and **Catalogs** (Enterprise) put the item in
  catalog sections. Order lead, schedule, lot size, unit weight and volume,
  perishable and serialized are for information only.

## Bill of materials

A product's bill of materials lists the items (products or services) that make it,
with quantities. An item can't be part of its own bill of materials, directly or
through another item. Each SKU of the item chooses which components it uses, on its
own **Bill of Materials** tab. See [SKUs](I004.md).

## Clone and Create SKU

- **Clone** copies the item, its translations and bill of materials into a new,
  unsaved item marked "(copy)". Attachments aren't copied.
- **Create SKU** starts a new SKU for this product.
