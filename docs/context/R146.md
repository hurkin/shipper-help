# Copper Plate Usage

Lists the copper plates used on fumigated containers, for fumigation records.

## What's included

- **Containers with a copper plate number** and an actual fumigation time within the
  dates.
- Sorted by copper plate.

## Things to know

- The copper plate wording printed comes from your database's *CUPLATE* setting.
