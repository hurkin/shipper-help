# Booking Documents

Documentation is the paperwork a shipment needs: phytosanitary certificates, bills
of lading, bank documents and so on. **Documentation tasks** list what each record
needs, and **documents** are the certificates and forms filled in inside Shipper from
a [document type](D002.md).

**Booking Documents** (under Documentation) lists the bookings with their documents
due and how many tasks are still open. Click a booking to open it on its **Tasks**
tab.

## Where tasks come from

Tasks flow down from the customer to the booking:

1. A company's **Documentation** tab lists the tasks it always needs.
2. A sales order, contract or quote takes the tasks of its customer, seller and
   ship-to when it opens, or when you save a change to those parties. Its
   **Documentation** tab can add more.
3. A booking takes the tasks of every order whose lines are on it (and of its
   bills) when it opens, or when you add a line or save its fulfillment.

Tasks passed down this way can't be removed from the order or booking, because
they would only come back. Change them where they come from. Tasks added by hand
can be removed.

## Tasks

- Each task has the number of copies for the buyer, seller, ship-to and bank,
  who gets the **originals**, whether to **include the LC number**, and comments.
  Double-click a shaded cell to change it.
- On a booking, tick **Complete** when the document is done. Your name and the time
  are recorded when you save. Untick it to clear them.
- Task changes are saved with the record's **Save**.

## Documents

- The booking's **Documents** tab lists its documents. **Task Complete** is ticked
  when a completed task names the document's type.
- **Add Document** opens the document. Choose the type (only types for bookings
  are offered). The number starts as the booking number.
- **Fill Fields** gathers values from the booking using the document type's SQL. It
  fills each field it has a value for, and can set the document number.
- Fields with a default value start with it. Anything still blank is typed in.
- **Save** saves the document straight away and keeps it open. Its type can't be
  changed once saved.
- **Reports** prints the document. It lists the reports for the document's type and
  opens over the document. Close the report to return to it. Save your changes
  first.
- Removing a document from the booking takes effect when you save the booking.
- Documents are system-generated forms. General files belong under
  **Attachments** instead.
- **A record with documents can't be deleted.** Once a document exists for a booking
  or an invoice, delete the document first.

## Permissions

- This permission covers Booking Documents and the Tasks, Documents and
  Documentation tabs. Read shows them, Write changes tasks and documents, and
  Delete removes documents.
- Anyone with it also needs at least Read on Bookings to open a booking.
- A posted booking's tasks and documents are read-only, except to an owner.
