# Custom Fields

Custom fields add your own fields to records: a grade, a color, an expiry date, a
moisture reading. Define them here, then attach them to categories, items and
inspection types.

## Things to know

- **Mnemonic** stands for the field everywhere, including custom reports. Don't
  change it once the field is in use. The **Description** can change as often as
  you like.
- **Type** decides what kind of value the field holds. For drop-down types, the
  **Lookup values** are the choices offered. Each choice can be translated.
- **Where fields appear:**
  - On a **category**, they appear on every item in it, and on those items' SKUs,
    agreement lines and work orders.
  - On an **inspection type**, they appear on inspections of that type.
  - Each assignment can be marked required, and ordered.
- **Values are entered on the Characteristics tab** of the item, SKU, agreement
  line or work order, and save with it. A required field that is empty stops the
  save and opens the tab (a checkbox is never required). Choosing another item or
  category shows its fields instead.
- **Standard reports don't show custom fields.** Custom reports can include them.
- Descriptions can be translated for users working in other languages.
- A custom field can be deleted only while nothing uses it. Otherwise make it
  inactive. Inactive fields still show where they are already used.
