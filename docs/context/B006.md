# Traffic Center

The Traffic Center is the ShipperXL Desktop dispatch console for loads coming in,
going out and being processed. From it you find a load and open it, or create a new
cargo.

## Movement types

| Movement | Means |
|---|---|
| Inbound | Product is coming into the facility |
| Outbound | Product is leaving the facility |
| Inbound and outbound | Product ships straight from the supplier |
| Neither | Product is processed at the facility |

## Things to know

- **Set the filters first:** work zone, location or work center, and movement type.
  They narrow the lists and become the defaults for a new cargo.
- **Incomplete loads** are cargo still missing something: a final weight, or a load,
  unload or ready-to-ship time.
- **Planned or incomplete work orders** are work orders waiting for their cargo to
  arrive.
- If a load isn't on either list, click **Create Cargo**.
- Searching matches cargo numbers and other load details anywhere in the text you
  type.

In the web app, the same work is done in **Inbound > Inbound Cargo** and
**Outbound > Outbound Cargo** (look at **Incomplete**), and **Processing > Work
Orders**.
