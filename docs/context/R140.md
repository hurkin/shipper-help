# Shipment Schedule

Lists sales order lines by work zone and processor, with their bookings, due dates,
SKUs and how much product is on hand or still at the supplier.

## What's included

- **Sales order lines,** once per assignment. A line on no booking appears as
  unassigned.
- **Canceled bookings are left out.**
- With due dates chosen, only lines on bookings whose cargo due or documents due
  falls within them.
- Narrowed by processor and work zone. Lines with neither appear only when the
  not-assigned choice is included.
- Narrowed by queue: bookings in the queues chosen, and, if the no-queue choice is
  included, bookings in none of them.
- **Completed orders aren't left out.** Use the due dates to limit the list.

## Things to know

- **Due** is the documents due date, else cargo due, else the order's requested
  delivery, else its order date. The label says which.
- The customer is hidden when the processor's company record says not to disclose
  the buyer.
- On hand and at supplier are shown in the line's unit (such as containers): units
  divided by the SKU's capacity factor. At supplier is ordered minus fulfilled on
  purchase contracts.
- Fulfilled counts cargo on the same booking, for the same work zone or processor.
