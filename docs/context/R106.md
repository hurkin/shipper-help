# Cargo Bill of Lading

Prints the bill of lading for one load: the booking's vessel and ports, the
container, its seal, weights and what it carries.

## What's included

- Run for a cargo, a scale ticket (its cargo), or a work order.
- **A cargo with exactly one outbound work order prints as that work order.** The
  worker and references then come from the work order.
- Weights are used only from scale tickets with **both gross and tare weighed**.
  Otherwise the weight is blank.
- The contents are the cargo's contents lines. A work order with no contents shows
  its inventory instead: what was added, or else what was taken out.
- Booking details come from the cargo's booking, or the work order's booking.

## Things to know

- Worker is the work order's worker, or else the drayman on the scale ticket.
- The release number is the booking's empty release number, or else the booking
  number.
- **Verified gross** is the net weight plus the container's own tare (pounds, or
  else kilograms), in your database's weight unit.
- Without a cargo, the cargo number reads *W/O* and the work order number.
