# Send Pictures

Send Pictures takes photos with the device and attaches them to a cargo or SKU,
such as photos of damaged bales or a loaded container.

## Things to know

- Choose the cargo or SKU, take or pick the pictures, and press **SAVE**.
- The pictures show on the record's attachments in Shipper.
- Sending by email needs the *Send an email to add attachments to Shipper* feature
  turned on for your database, and an email app set up on the device. On Android,
  choose Gmail and tap **ALWAYS**. If you picked the wrong app, reset it in the
  device's **Settings > Apps > Reset app preferences**.
- This feature can only add pictures. It can't open or delete attachments.
