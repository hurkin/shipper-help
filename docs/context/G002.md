# Ports

Ports are the sea, air, rail and road locations used on bookings, price and cost
lists and carrier contracts. The list is kept up to date for you, with quarterly
updates.

## Things to know

- **You can only switch a port on or off.** Port details (codes, coordinates,
  maximum cargo weights) are maintained centrally.
- The list is filtered by country (the United States to start with) and region.
- **Port groups** stand for several ports. A price or cost list line for a group
  applies to every port in it.
- If a port is missing, email support@shipperxl.com.
