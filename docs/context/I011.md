# Physical Inventory

Physical Inventory is a worksheet of counted quantities at one point in time. You
compare it with what Shipper says is on hand, then correct inventory where needed.

## Things to know

- **A worksheet is the counts stamped with exactly one Checked date and time.**
  Counts at any other time are a different worksheet. Checked starts at the latest
  count in the system, or the end of last month if there are none.
- **Rebuild** replaces the counts the filters show with what Shipper has on hand as
  of Checked. Counts at other times, or outside the filters, are left alone.
  Completed SKUs and zero balances are left out.
- Filter by work zone, location owner, location and SKU. **Find in worksheet**
  searches what is loaded.
- **Raw** is required on every row. Edit raw, processed and memo in the grid, then
  save them together.
- Rows you add are stamped with Checked.
- **Counts don't change inventory.** Use reports such as **Physical Inventory** and
  **Physical Inventory Cost Variance** to compare, then correct the inventory itself.

## Who can do what

Read views the worksheet. Write edits the counts. Delete adds and removes rows and
rebuilds.
