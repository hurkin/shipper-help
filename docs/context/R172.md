# Item Price History

Lists the prices agreed for items over time, on the agreements you choose, with the
booking each line went on.

## What's included

- Agreement lines on agreements **ordered** within the dates (whole days in your time
  zone), of the types chosen, for the items chosen.
- Only agreements where a chosen company is the buyer or seller.
- One commodity at a time. Choose the not-assigned commodity to see items with none.

## Things to know

- Booking details (sail date, ports) come from a booking the line is assigned to.
  If several, one is shown.
