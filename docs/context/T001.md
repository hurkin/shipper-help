# Assets

An asset is equipment or property: a vehicle, a registered tool or machine, or a
piece of land. Assets are under **Lists > Assets**.

## The list

- It starts with active assets. Switch to **Inactive** or **All**, and narrow it by
  **Asset Class**.
- The search finds part of an asset's description.

## Things to know

- **The asset class decides the fields.** The class can't be changed once the asset
  is saved.
  - **Vehicle:** license number and authority, VIN, tracking number, odometer and
    hour meter.
  - **Registered:** serial number.
  - **Land:** one of the party's addresses (choose the party first), a location and
    acres.
- Asset class and **Description** are required.
- **An asset can belong to a party.** On a scale ticket, the **Vehicle** list
  shows the drayman's own vehicles first.
- The tabs **Work Orders**, **Scale Tickets**, **Agreement Items**, **Bill Items**
  and **SKUs** list the records that use the asset, each with its count. You see a
  tab only if you can open those records.
- **Clone** starts a new asset with the same class and details, its description
  marked *(copy)*.
- **Delete** is refused while anything uses the asset. Clear **Active** instead to
  retire it.
- Attachments, such as photos or registration papers, can be added once the asset
  is saved.

## Where an asset is chosen

With rights to assets, you can choose an active asset on a
[work order](W001.md) (beside **Shipment**), an agreement line, a bill line and a
[SKU](I004.md). Without them, the field is hidden and an asset already saved there
is left alone. A [scale ticket](B004.md)'s **Vehicle** is a vehicle asset, chosen
the same way.
