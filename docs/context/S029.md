# Freight Classes

Freight classes are the industry's classes for freight rates. Ask your freight
forwarder, the carrier or another knowledgeable party which applies to your
products.

## Things to know

- A commodity can name its freight class.
- Carrier service contract rates can be set per freight class.
- Freight classes are deactivated, not deleted.
