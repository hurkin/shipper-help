# Cargo

A cargo is one physical load: a container, flatbed, pickup or railcar. It holds
the load's scale ticket, work orders, the agreement lines it fulfills, its
inventory and, when outbound, its contents.

## Movement type

The movement type decides what the screen offers:

| Movement | Inventory tab | Dates shown |
|---|---|---|
| Inbound | Find a purchase line by SKU, add tags | Received, Unloaded, Received by Carrier |
| Outbound | Deduct by tag or by SKU and location; Contents tab | Received, Loaded, Ready to Ship, Delivered to Carrier, Delivered by Carrier |
| Both (direct ship) or neither (process) | Both ways | All |

**Carrier Timeline** dates show only when the cargo is on a booking. **Ready to
Ship**, under **Processor Timeline**, is when the load is planned to be ready to go,
not something that happened.

Typical loads:

- **Inbound:** usually a flatbed with a manifest. Print inventory tags, one per
  block.
- **Outbound:** usually a container with a seal number. Print a cargo bill of
  lading.
- **Direct ship from the supplier:** usually a container with a manifest. Loaded
  and ready to ship are the same time. Print a cargo bill of lading.
- **Process:** inventory moved or processed at your site.

## The grid

- **Received:** received from the carrier, but not yet loaded or unloaded.
- **Incomplete:** not yet loaded (outbound) or unloaded (inbound). Includes cargo
  with no dates at all.
- **Finished:** loaded or unloaded within the dates.
- Cargo with a **Completed** date shows only with **Include completed**.
- A search that finds nothing looks across all cargo and says why each match was
  outside your view.

## Bulk Edit

**Bulk Edit** changes several cargo at once, such as fumigating or opening a row of
containers. It replaces ShipperXL Desktop's Fumigation Tool, and is on the
**Inbound**, **Outbound** and **Process Cargo** lists.

1. Click **Bulk Edit**, then tick the cargo to change, or click a row to tick it.
   Ticks are kept when you change views or search again.
2. Click **Edit N Selected**. The dialog follows the cargo's General tab:
   **Container Type**, **Movement Type**, **Location**, **Carrier Status**,
   **Ready to Ship**, **Fumigated**, **Opened**, **Tested** and **Memo**.
3. Fill in only the values to change, then click **Update**.
4. Each cargo's result is listed: Saved, Partly saved, Failed or Skipped. Cargo that
   wasn't saved stays ticked, so you can try again. Click **Done** to leave Bulk
   Edit.

- **Only the values you fill in are changed.** Blank values are left alone, so Bulk
  Edit can't clear a value. **Memo** replaces each cargo's memo.
- **Fumigated**, **Opened** and **Tested** are set only on containers, and **Ready
  to Ship** only on outbound and process cargo. A container type or movement type
  set in the same edit counts. The others are listed as not set, with why.
- Cargo on a posted booking is skipped.
- Bulk Edit needs write permission on cargo.

## Things to know

- A new cargo takes the next cargo number. A container type is required.
- **Container numbers are checked.** For container types whose class is a
  container, Shipper checks the ISO check digit and shows the right one if it is
  wrong. Seal, copper plate and tare are entered exactly as printed on the box.
- **Location** is where the cargo is. Any active location can be chosen.
- **Carrier Status**, under Tare Kilograms, is the carrier's status for this
  container. Watching the shipline updates it.
- **Shipment** is the booking. Type part of its number to find it, or choose **New
  booking** to create one there and then. The button in the field opens the booking
  in a new tab.
- **A posted booking locks its cargo.** The cargo can't be changed once its booking
  is posted.
- Cargo numbers can repeat, because containers are reused. Lookups show each
  match's created date and booking.

## Fulfillment tab

- A cargo with no lines of its own shows its booking's lines, with blank
  quantities. Enter a quantity to put the line on the cargo.
- **Add line item** searches purchase lines for inbound cargo and sales lines for
  outbound cargo, by agreement number, SKU or reference.
- Setting a quantity to 0 removes that row.
- **Party** is the other side of the agreement: a purchase's seller, or a sale's
  customer.
- **Agreement** names the agreement's type and number, such as *Purchase Order
  1234*. Click it to open that agreement on its Lines tab with the line open
  (if you can open that type of agreement).
- A sale is usually fulfilled with 1 (one container). A purchase is usually
  fulfilled in units.

## Inventory tab

- Rows are dated with **Unloaded** (else **Received**) inbound, and **Loaded** (else
  **Delivered to Carrier**) outbound, or now if neither is set.
- **Add tags** creates up to 99 tags per cargo. They take the SKU you chose (at 0),
  or split each fulfilled purchase line's quantity evenly across them.
- A tag with no location isn't in inventory until it is received.
- Scanning a tag outbound deducts its whole balance from where it is. A SKU and
  source location deducts everything that SKU holds there.
- Unit weights come from this cargo's scale ticket when it is inbound: net weight
  divided by the units received.

## Scale Ticket tab

The cargo's **Save** saves its scale ticket too, so there is one Save for both. See
[Scale Tickets](B004.md).

## Contents tab (outbound)

**Copy from inventory** makes one line for everything shipped. **Copy from order**
makes one line per SKU, described by the customer SKU. Contents print on documents
and can be used for invoice descriptions.

## Inspections tab

The inspections that include this cargo, newest first. Click one to open it.
**Add Inspection** starts a new one with this cargo on it. See
[Inspections](I008.md).
