# Bank Sight Draft

Prints a sight draft on the bank for an invoice paid by letter of credit.

## What's included

- Run for one **invoice**.
- The bank details, drawee, drafts terms and notes come from the **letter of credit
  the invoice points to.** Without one, those parts are blank.
- The amount is the invoice total, also spelled out in words.

## Things to know

- The draft is addressed to the letter of credit's notify party, or else its issuing
  bank.
- The date is the planned departure of a booking linked to the invoice.
