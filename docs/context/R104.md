# Bill

Prints a bill, invoice, credit memo or letter of credit.

## What's included

- Run for one bill, from its **Reports** button.
- **Only bills with at least one line print.** A bill with no lines comes out blank.
- Lines print their own item code and description, not the item's current ones.
- Terms, Incoterm, currency and units are shown in your language when translated.

## Things to know

- Quantities, secondary quantities and the currency round to the number of places
  set on their units, or 3 when a unit isn't rounded.
- The addresses printed are the bill's saved address blocks.
- **Pro Forma** is shown when the bill is marked pro forma.
