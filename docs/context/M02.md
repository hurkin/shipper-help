# Move

Move puts inventory in a new location by scanning. It is the mobile app's version
of [Move Inventory](I010.md) and follows the same rules.

## Things to know

- **Press ADD for each line, then DONE.** Nothing is saved until you press
  **DONE**. A line you didn't **ADD** isn't moved.
- **A tag moves whole.** Scan the tag and the new location. All of its quantity
  goes there.
- **A SKU tag needs more.** Enter the **FROM** location and the raw or processed
  quantity to move. You can't move more than is there.
- **Everything or nothing.** If any line fails, nothing moves and the app shows
  why: not enough on hand, location not found, or the same from and to location.
- Moves are dated with the time you press **DONE**. Record a move as it happens,
  or the balances in between are wrong.
