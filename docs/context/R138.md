# Invoice Summary

Lists invoices in a period with their totals and payments.

## What's included

- **Invoices only,** dated within the dates.
- **Only invoices with lines.**
- Run for a company, it lists invoices where the company is the buyer, seller or
  ship-to.
- Options limit it to invoices not completed, not posted, not fully paid, or overdue.
- **Overdue** means past due and not fully paid. **An invoice with no due date always
  counts as overdue,** even when paid.

## Things to know

- The ship-to country is the invoice's, or else the destination country of a booking
  linked to it.
- Payments are shown once per invoice.
