# Bookings

A booking is a reservation with a carrier: vessel, ports and dates. *Booking* and
*shipment* mean the same record. The booking number from the shipline identifies it.

## Creating one

- A booking needs a number and a carrier. Leave the number blank to take the next
  booking number.
- **Booking Class** starts at Ocean Vessel each time; pick another class if the
  booking isn't by sea. New bookings start in the booking starting queues.
- **Watch Shipline for changes** is ticked by default if your database is set up
  that way, except when the number was generated.

## The four legs

The **General** tab lays the voyage out as four legs, left to right, under a band
of arrows:

| Leg | Means |
|---|---|
| **IPI** | Place of receipt |
| **POL** | Port of loading, with its terminal |
| **POD** | Port of discharge, with its terminal |
| **DLV** | Place of delivery |

- Under each leg are its location, terminal (POL and POD), its cut-off (IPI and
  POL), and the planned date with its actual beside it: departs for IPI and POL,
  arrives for POD and DLV. Each cut-off sits just above its departure. Empty pickup
  (under IPI) and receiving (under POL) come after the dates.
- Each arrow shows the time to the next leg, in calendar days: IPI departs to POL
  departs, POL departs to POD arrives, and POD arrives to DLV arrives. It uses the
  actual dates where they are entered, otherwise the planned ones. It reads **same
  day** when the dates match, and turns red when the dates are out of order.
- A booking in a class other than ocean has only **IPI** and **DLV**: no ports of
  loading or discharge, vessel, voyage, containers, empty pickup or receiving. Its
  **Cargo Due** is under IPI, above Departs, and the arrow shows IPI departs to DLV
  arrives. Changing an ocean booking to another class warns that saving deletes its
  ocean details.
- On a phone the legs are listed one under another, with the time to the next leg
  between them.
- A port with no terminals says so in its **Terminal** field.
- **Empty Equipment** is where empty equipment is picked up, and **Full
  Equipment** where loaded equipment is returned.
- **Shipper** is the company officially recognized as the shipper on the booking.
- **Memo** prints on several reports, some of which go to partners. Keep sensitive
  notes in **Internal Memo**.
- **Reference 1** and **Reference 2**, under the memos, hold other parties' numbers
  for the booking.

Sail and arrival dates are the port's calendar days. They are shown exactly as
entered and never converted to your time zone. Other times (documents due,
completed, posted) are shown in your time zone.

## Clone

**Clone** makes a new booking from this one and opens it. Enter the new booking
number, or leave it blank for the next number. The clone copies the parties, ports,
dates and sea details. The bill of lading, freight cost, empty release, vessel,
voyage, Reference 1 and 2, Completed and Posted are left blank. Lines, cargo, notes,
documents and attachments stay with this booking. Save your changes first: the
clone is made from what is saved.

## The grid

| View | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Loading** | Sails today or later, or has no sail date |
| **Departing** | Has sailed, and delivers today or later |
| **Arriving** | Delivered in the last 30 days, up to yesterday |
| **Completed** | Completed in the last 30 days |

These are the default dates. Each view's dates can be changed. A completed booking
shows only under Completed. Canceled bookings are hidden unless you include them.

## Tabs

- **Freight:** the shipper, forwarder, cut-off dates, freight details and the
  containers (see [Freight rate](B008.md?id=freight-rate) below). **Add cargo**
  creates a container on this booking. It picks up the booking's lines: a
  sales line priced per container is fulfilled with 1, and every other line is put
  on at 0.
- **Fulfillment:** the agreement lines on this booking. **Add line item** puts a
  line on at 0. Then enter the assigned quantity. The Cargo columns show what the
  containers have assigned and fulfilled. **Party** is a purchase's seller or a
  sale's customer. **Work Zone**, **Work Center** and **External Processor** are
  edited in place. Click a line's **Agreement** (such as *Sales Order 1234*) to open
  that line on its agreement.
- **Tasks:** the documentation tasks for this booking, from its orders and added by
  hand. Tick **Complete** as each document is done.
- **Documents:** the certificates and forms produced for it. **Add Document** fills
  one in. See [Booking Documents](D001.md).
- **Invoices:** the booking's **Letter of Credit**, the invoices and bills linked
  to the booking, and the two **Create Invoice** buttons. Choose the letter of
  credit and save before creating an invoice: the invoice then points to it, and
  the booking takes its documentation tasks. A booking has one letter of credit.
  See [Create Invoices](../instructions/create-invoices.md) and
  [Letters of Credit](IV07.md).

- **Notes:** click a note to read the whole of it. See
  [Notes](../instructions/getting-started.md?id=notes).

Tasks and Documents need the documents permission. Opening a booking from
**Booking Documents** starts on Tasks.

## Freight rate

The **Freight** tab's **Estimated Freight Rate** lists the carrier's contract rates
that fit the booking, newest first, each as the contract number, the estimated rate
and the rate's memo. Pick one to keep it on the booking. A rate fits when:

- its contract is with the booking's carrier and in force on the POL departure date;
- it runs from the booking's **IPI** location to its **DLV** location;
- it is for the booking's container class, and for its container type or any type;
- it is for the booking's **Freight Class**, or for no particular class.

Without a carrier, IPI and DLV locations, POL departure and container class, the
field says what is missing. The estimate adds the contract's fees, then the rate's
own fees, to the base rate. A rate picked earlier stays on the booking even when it
no longer fits, until you pick another or choose **None**. **Actual Freight Cost**,
under it, is what the carrier charged. See [Carrier Service Contracts](B009.md).

## Watching the shipline

There is a small one-time fee per booking watched.

Changes from the carrier are applied as they arrive: vessel, voyage, ports, dates,
status, and each container's type and carrier dates. A note lists what changed, and
the booking goes into the remote-changes queue. Once the shipline service has
picked up the booking, the box can't be unticked.

## Key reports

- **Booking Cargo:** the containers, contents, weights, orders and booking details.
  Often sent to partners.
- **Estimated Shrink:** product received, processed and shipped, for an estimate of
  shrink. See [Estimated Shrink](R153.md).
- **Processor Instruction:** for sending a booking to another company to process.
- **Shipment Inventory Cost:** the cost of the product used, for accounting.

## Completed, Posted and Delete

- **Completing a booking needs delete rights** (or an owner).
- With accounting integration, completed bookings are picked up and posted. A
  posted booking and all its cargo are read-only. Only an owner can change the
  Posted date.
- A booking can be deleted only if it isn't posted and has no cargo, work orders,
  fulfilled lines, invoices, bills or documents. Its assignments, notes and
  attachments go with it.
