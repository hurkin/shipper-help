# Bookings

A booking is a reservation with a carrier: vessel, ports and dates. *Booking* and
*shipment* mean the same record. The booking number from the shipline identifies it.

## Creating one

- A booking needs a number and a carrier. Leave the number blank to take the next
  booking number.
- New bookings are ocean bookings, and start in the booking starting queues.
- **Watch Shipline for changes** is ticked by default if your database is set up
  that way, except when the number was generated.

## The four legs

| Leg | Means |
|---|---|
| **IPI** | Place of receipt |
| **POL** | Port of loading, with its terminal |
| **POD** | Port of discharge, with its terminal |
| **DLV** | Place of delivery |

Sail and arrival dates are the port's calendar days. They are shown exactly as
entered and never converted to your time zone. Other times (documents due,
completed, posted) are shown in your time zone.

## The grid

| View | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Loading** | Sails today or later, or has no sail date |
| **Departing** | Has sailed, and delivers today or later |
| **Arriving** | Delivered in the last 30 days, up to yesterday |
| **Completed** | Completed in the last 30 days |

These are the default dates. Each view's dates can be changed. A completed booking
shows only under Completed. Canceled bookings are hidden unless you include them.

## Tabs

- **Freight:** the shipper, forwarder, cut-off dates and the containers. **Add
  cargo** creates a container on this booking. It picks up the booking's lines: a
  sales line priced per container is fulfilled with 1, and every other line is put
  on at 0.
- **Fulfillment:** the agreement lines on this booking. **Add line item** puts a
  line on at 0. Then enter the assigned quantity. The Cargo columns show what the
  containers have assigned and fulfilled.
- **Tasks:** the documentation tasks for this booking, from its orders and added by
  hand. Tick **Complete** as each document is done.
- **Documents:** the certificates and forms produced for it. **Add Document** fills
  one in. See [Booking Documents](D001.md).
- **Invoices:** the booking's **Letter of Credit**, the invoices and bills linked
  to the booking, and the two **Create Invoice** buttons. Choose the letter of
  credit and save before creating an invoice: the invoice then points to it, and
  the booking takes its documentation tasks. A booking has one letter of credit.
  See [Create Invoices](../instructions/create-invoices.md) and
  [Letters of Credit](IV07.md).

Tasks and Documents need the documents permission. Opening a booking from
**Booking Documents** starts on Tasks.

## Watching the shipline

Changes from the carrier are applied as they arrive: vessel, voyage, ports, dates,
status, and each container's type and carrier dates. A note lists what changed, and
the booking goes into the remote-changes queue. Once the shipline service has
picked up the booking, the box can't be unticked.

## Completed, Posted and Delete

- **Completing a booking needs delete rights** (or an owner).
- With accounting integration, completed bookings are picked up and posted. A
  posted booking and all its cargo are read-only. Only an owner can change the
  Posted date.
- A booking can be deleted only if it isn't posted and has no cargo, work orders,
  fulfilled lines, invoices, bills or documents. Its assignments, notes and
  attachments go with it.
