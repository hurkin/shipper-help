# Credit Memos

A credit memo reduces what a customer owes, for example after a claim. Like an
invoice, your company is the seller and the customer the buyer.

## Things to know

- The number comes from the credit memo number scheme, and a new credit memo starts
  in the credit memo starting queues.
- **Terms** for a new credit memo are, in order: terms whose short code is *CREDIT*,
  the customer's terms, your company's terms, or else the active terms with the
  fewest days due. **Payment Terms** can be changed, and move Due, as on
  [invoices](IV01.md).
- Lines work as on invoices: they print their own item code and description, an
  empty amount saves as 0, and **Agreement Line** and **Cargo** link a line to a
  sales line and a cargo. Amounts aren't filled from a price list.
- A credit memo can be created automatically when an invoice is completed. See
  [Automated Credit Memos](../instructions/automated-credit-memos.md).
- Payments, completing, posting and deleting follow the same rules as
  [invoices](IV01.md). Posting a payment starts any *Credit Memo Payment Posted*
  scheduled jobs.
