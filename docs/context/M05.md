# Ship Inventory

Ship Inventory takes inventory out against a work order by scanning, without
opening the cargo, like the mobile app's Ship screen.

## Things to know

- **Choose the work order first.** The rows are recorded on its cargo, or on the
  work order if it has no cargo.
- **A tag goes whole,** from wherever it is. A tag with nothing left on it is
  refused.
- **A SKU or location** shows what is on hand in each location. Choose how much raw
  and processed to take from each.
- **A SKU deduction needs a location,** and can't take more than is there.
- **Everything or nothing.** Every line is checked first. If any line fails, nothing
  is shipped and the failing lines show why.
- Rows are dated now. Shipping starts the work order if it hadn't started.

## In the mobile app

- Ship takes product out of inventory as it is loaded. It can take raw product
  and convert it on the way (enter both raw and processed), or take processed
  product staged earlier with [Process](M04.md).
- **Enter the processed quantity and choose whether to finish the work order
  before you press ADD.**
- **Enter one processed value for the load.** For 896 units, enter 896 once
  rather than eight lines of 112.
- A SKU tag is usual for processed product, which no longer has its own tags.
  Enter the **FROM** location and the processed quantity.
- Press **DONE** to save. Nothing is saved until you do.
