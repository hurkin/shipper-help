# Markets

A market is a group of customers or suppliers you sell to or buy from in the same
way, such as a set of countries or regions.

## Things to know

- A company is put in a market on its own record.
- **Markets rank price and cost lists.** A list linked to a company's market wins
  over lists that aren't, whatever else matches. A list linked to other markets
  still applies to everyone else, just with lower rank. Markets never stop a list
  from applying.
- Markets are deactivated, not deleted.
