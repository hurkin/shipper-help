# Sales Summary

Summarizes Sales orders and contracts as of a date, with orders shown under the contracts they are
drawn from: ordered, ordered by child orders, assigned, fulfilled, and estimated and
actual values.

## What's included

- **Sales orders and contracts** only, not canceled.
- Choose a status:
  - **Open:** not completed, in force on the date, and not fully fulfilled (counting
    orders drawn from contracts).
  - **Active:** in force on the date, completed or not.
  - **Expired:** not completed and not fully fulfilled, with an expiry date on or
    after the date.
- **In force** means the date falls between the effective date (or the day before
  ordering) and the expiry date. **With no expiry date, an agreement counts as in force
  for two years after it was ordered.**
- Run for a company: agreements where it is the buyer or seller.
- Product lines must have a commodity among those chosen. Service lines are always
  included.
- The work zone is the line SKU's default work zone. A line with no SKU or no default
  work zone appears only when the not-assigned choice is included.

## Things to know

- An order drawn from a contract that is also listed appears under that contract.
- **Not ordered** (contracts only) is the contract quantity less what child orders
  have taken, for lines not completed.
- **Not fulfilled** is assigned minus fulfilled, or ordered minus fulfilled when
  nothing is assigned.
- Completed lines use their actual value. Others use the estimated value.
- The party shown is the buyer.
