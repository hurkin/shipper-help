# Physical Inventory Cost Variance

Compares estimated and actual costs for the SKUs in a physical count: the costs you
expected on their purchase lines against the bills actually received.

## What's included

- **SKUs counted at the chosen Checked time,** narrowed by the SKU's default work
  zone. A SKU with no default work zone appears only when the not-assigned choice is
  included.
- For each purchase order or contract line on those SKUs (optionally for one seller):
  - **Product:** the line's quantity and estimated value.
  - **Estimated costs:** each variable cost on the line, per unit × the line's quantity,
    by cost category and who it is payable to.
  - **Actual costs:** supplier bill lines drawn from the purchase line, by the bill
    item's category and supplier.

## Things to know

- Actual costs come only from bills whose lines are tied to the purchase line. Bills
  entered without that link don't appear.
