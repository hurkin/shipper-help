# Order Status

Shows where every open (or recently finished) agreement line stands: ordered,
assigned, fulfilled, its booking and dates, invoice and payment dates, and queues.

## What's included

- Agreement lines that **aren't fully fulfilled,** plus lines whose first fulfillment
  came after the earliest date (so recently finished lines still show).
- Agreements **ordered** on or after the earliest date, or on a booking sailing on or
  after it.
- **Canceled agreements are left out.**
- Agreements of the types chosen, with a chosen company as buyer or seller.
- **Both the buyer and the seller must be companies.** An agreement with a contact
  (person) as buyer or seller doesn't appear.
- **Only items with a commodity among those chosen.**
- Narrowed by the assignment's work zone and processor. A line not on a booking needs
  the not-assigned choices for both.
- Narrowed by the booking's forwarder. Lines with no booking are always included.

## Things to know

- A line assigned to several bookings appears once for each.
- Invoice and payment dates are shown for invoices linked to the agreement and to the
  booking separately.
