# Supplier Performance

Follows each supplier's SKUs from purchase to sale: tons received and shipped, purchase
and sale prices, costs, invoices and bookings.

## What's included

- SKUs bought on purchase lines effective within the last few years, or sold on sales
  orders placed within them (two years by default).
- Run for a supplier or a SKU.
- Rows where the raw quantity nets to zero are left out.
- Results come 250 rows at a time.

## Things to know

- **The figures come from a reporting copy of your data, refreshed by a system
  scheduled job.** The report shows when it was last refreshed. Recent changes appear
  after the next refresh.
- Tons are short tons or metric tons by your database's weight unit.
- Sales figures come from the bookings the SKU's inventory went out on.
