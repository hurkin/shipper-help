# Queue Statistics

Shows how each queue is performing: how many items are waiting, how long they have
waited, and how long items have taken to move through it in the past.

## What's included

- **Active queues that have at least one item in them now.** An empty queue doesn't
  appear.
- Current figures: items waiting, their average age in days, and when items were
  first and last added.
- Historical figures (average, shortest, longest and spread of days in the queue)
  come from the queue's change history.

## Things to know

- An item's age counts from when it was first added, not from when it reached this
  queue through **Next Queue**.
