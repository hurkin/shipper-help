# Work Order Summary

Summarizes work orders: planned against actual start and finish, hours taken, the
container's weight and SKU. Use it to review the work on each container of a
booking, or throughput over a period.

## What's included

- Work orders **finished** within the dates (whole days in your time zone). Run for a
  booking, it lists every work order on the booking or its cargo, finished or not.
- Narrowed by work zone, work center and work item type. A work order with none
  appears only when the not-assigned choice is included.
- Worker: choose all workers, particular ones, or work orders with no worker.

## Things to know

- Start and finish deltas are actual minus planned, in hours. Actual hours are
  finish minus start.
- Weight is the cargo's scale ticket net, in tons. With no ticket, a work order that
  took raw product out and made processed product shows the processed tons.
- The SKU shows only when the cargo (or work order) carries a single SKU.
- Moisture is the average of the *MOISLO* and *MOISHI* fields on the cargo's
  inspections.
