# Traffic Audit

Lists cargo created or changed in a period and flags what looks incomplete, to catch
data entry problems.

## What's included

- Cargo **created or last changed** within the dates (whole days in your time zone).
- User filter: who created or last changed the cargo, exactly.
- Narrowed by movement type, scale, cargo location, and SKU (cargo with inventory of
  that exact SKU).

## The flags

- **Missing weights:** the scale ticket lacks a gross or tare weight. A cargo with no
  scale ticket is flagged too.
- **Ticket mismatch (\*):** the cargo points to one scale ticket while another ticket
  points to the cargo.
- **Unfinished work orders:** work orders on the cargo with no actual finish.
- **No worker:** work orders with no worker, where the service requires one.
- **Mismatched costs:** work orders whose rate differs from the cost list line they
  came from.

## Things to know

- Inventory totals count rows with a location only.
- The work zone is the cargo location's, else the scale's.
