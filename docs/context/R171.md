# Open Purchase Summary

Lists agreement lines that aren't fully fulfilled, with the remaining quantity,
estimated weight and value, by commodity, item and work zone.

## What's included

- Agreements of the types chosen, with at least one line where **ordered is more than
  fulfilled**. Only those lines are shown.
- **In force on the chosen date:** the date falls between the agreement's effective
  date (or the day before it was ordered) and its expiry (or a year after it was
  ordered).
- **Expired only:** instead, agreements whose expiry (or a year after ordering) is
  before the date. Lines on SKUs completed by then are left out.
- Run for a company: agreements where it is the buyer or seller.
- **Only product items,** and only those whose commodity (or the SKU's item's) is
  among the commodities chosen.
- The work zone is the line SKU's default work zone. A line with no SKU or no
  default work zone appears only when the not-assigned choice is included.

## Things to know

- The unit is the SKU's raw unit, or else the line's unit.
- **Estimated weight** applies only to lines priced per a weight unit: the estimated
  value divided by the price (or the estimated quantity when the price is 0).
- The party shown is the seller.
