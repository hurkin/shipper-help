# Bills

A bill is what you owe a supplier or worker. Your company is always the buyer and
the supplier the seller.

## Creating one

- **In bulk** from finished work orders or from purchase fulfillment, with
  [Create Bills](W005.md).
- **From a purchase order,** with **Create Bill** on the order. See
  [Purchase Orders](A002.md).
- **New, by hand:** you choose the supplier. The number comes from the bill number
  scheme, the bill date is today, and the bill starts in the bill starting queues.
- **Terms** for a new bill are, in order: terms whose short code is *BILL*, the
  supplier's terms, your company's terms, or else the active terms with the fewest
  days due. Change them under **Payment Terms** on **Product & Terms**.
- **Due** is the bill date plus the terms' days. Changing the bill date or the terms
  moves it. New terms also fill **Additional Terms** from their template when it is
  empty.

## Things to know

- **Address blocks** are the addresses printed on the bill. Picking a party or
  address rewrites its block, and you can still edit it.
- **Lines** print their own item code and description. Picking an item copies them.
  An empty amount saves as 0. The line numbers are assigned for you.
- **A line's amount fills from the cost list** when you change its item, Per or
  SKU, using the supplier and the bill date. See [Cost Lists](I005.md). Opening a
  saved line doesn't change its amount.
- **Agreement Line** links a line to the purchase order or contract line it bills.
  Search by agreement number, SKU or supplier SKU. **Cargo** links it to a cargo:
  search by cargo, booking, manifest, ticket, SKU or work order number.
- **Accounting codes** (item number, item class and expense account) are filled in
  each time the bill or a line is saved. The line shows them, read-only, once
  saved. Codes already on a line are kept, unless you change its item, which
  fills them again from the new item. See
  [How accounting codes are chosen](../instructions/accounting-integration.md?id=how-accounting-codes-are-chosen).
- **A line's description starts with its work zone,** such as *Ellensburg: Squeeze
  Loading*. Saving adds the zone again if it was removed.
- The **Related** tab lists every agreement tied to the bill.

## Clone

**Clone** makes a new bill from this one. A bill is always cloned to a bill, as an
invoice is to an invoice.

- Leave **New Bill Number** blank to take the next number, or type one.
- The new bill has this one's parties, terms and details, its lines and its notes,
  and is dated today, with Due moved by its terms.
- Its references and its ship, printed, confirmed, completed and posted dates are
  blank. Payments, links and attachments stay with this bill.
- Lines keep their agreement line and cargo, so check they aren't billed twice.
- It starts in the bill starting queues. Save your changes first.
- A letter of credit's clone also copies its bank parties and details, without its
  reference number, advice number or amendments.

## Grid filters

- **Open:** not fully paid, including new bills with no lines.
- **Completed:** completed within the dates.
- **Paid:** fully paid, by the date of the last payment.

## Payments

- Anyone who can edit bills can add or change a payment, even on a posted bill.
- Once a payment is posted, only an owner can change, unpost or delete it.
- Posting a payment starts any *Bill Payment Posted* scheduled jobs.

## Completed, Posted and Delete

- **Completed** starts any *Bill Completed* scheduled jobs. With accounting
  integration, completed bills are sent to accounting and posted. One dated in the
  future waits until its date.
- Once **Posted**, the bill and its lines are read-only. Only an owner can change the
  Posted date.
- A bill can be deleted only if it isn't posted and has no payments, posted lines,
  generated documents or inventory on its lines.
- **Deleting a bill frees its work orders.** They show in Create Bills again, so a
  wrong bill can be redone.
