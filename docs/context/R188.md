# Letter of Credit Status

Lists letters of credit in force on a date, with the invoices and bookings drawn
against each.

## What's included

- Letters of credit **in force on the chosen date:** from their issue date (or the
  day before they were entered) to their expiry date (or open-ended).
- Run for a buyer, or for one letter of credit.
- You can limit it to letters of credit that aren't completed, or that have no invoice
  yet.

## Things to know

- **An invoice counts against a letter of credit** only when it points to that letter
  of credit and is linked to the same booking the letter of credit is linked to.
- The credit amount is shown once per letter of credit, however many invoices it has.
- The invoice amount is worked out on the report: price × the secondary quantity
  converted to the pricing unit, or price × quantity.
- The advice reference is the advice number, else the reference number, else a
  reference on the letter of credit.
