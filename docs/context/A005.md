# Sales Contracts

A sales contract is an agreement to sell over a period of time, often a set
quantity of containers. Sales orders are drawn from it, and their fulfillment
counts towards it.

## Things to know

- You choose the customer. Your company is always the seller, and you are its sales
  agent if your sign-in email is one of the company's contacts.
- The number comes from the sales contract number scheme. A new contract starts in
  the sales contract starting queues, with your default terms templates in
  **Additional Terms**.
- A contract with no lines doesn't show in the grid.
- **Fulfilled** on a contract includes everything fulfilled on orders drawn from
  it. **Ordered** counts only the contract's own lines.
- A contract can't be drawn from another contract, so it has no contract picker.
- Putting a SKU on a contract line is how you set product aside for a customer. See
  [Reserve Inventory with a Sales Contract](../instructions/reserve-by-sales-contract.md).

## Lines

- **Unit Type** is Each or Container. **Container Type** appears only for
  Container.
- **Per** is limited to units of the item's selling unit class, or fixed when the
  item is sold only by its default unit.
- **Sales Amount** fills from the price list when you change the item, Per, SKU or
  container type. See [Price Lists](I006.md).
- **Assignments** put a line on a booking. Each needs a quantity, a booking and a
  work zone.

## Fields

The agreement's and lines' fields work as on [sales orders](A004.md?id=fields).

## Auto Work Orders

The **Auto Work Orders** tab lists work orders Shipper creates on a cargo as soon
as one of this contract's lines is assigned or fulfilled on it. Every line takes
them unless it is turned off on the line's own **Auto Work Orders** tab, where a
line can also have its own. See [Work Orders](W001.md).

## Drawing orders from the contract

Use **Copy To > Sales Order**. The new order is drawn from this contract, and each
of its lines points at the matching contract line. Prices carry over only when
copying to the same type. You can also pick the contract on an existing order.

## Completed and Posted

- **Completed** starts any *Sales Contract Completed* scheduled jobs. With
  accounting integration, completed contracts are picked up and posted.
- Once **Posted**, the contract is read-only. Only an owner can change the Posted
  date.

## Documentation

The **Documentation** tab lists the documentation tasks for this contract. It takes
its customer's, seller's and ship-to's tasks when it opens, or when you save a
change to them. Those can't be removed here. Add any others the contract needs. Its
bookings take these tasks. Save to keep changes. See [Booking Documents](D001.md).
