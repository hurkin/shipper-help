# Price Lists

A price list holds what you charge per unit of an item. Sales lines take their
**Sales Amount** from it.

## How a price is chosen

When you change a sales line's item, Per, SKU or container type, Shipper looks for a
price:

1. **The line must match exactly:** same item, and priced per the same unit.
2. **The list must be in force** on the agreement's order date. If the list also
   has ship dates (set in ShipperXL Desktop), today must fall within them.
3. **Every other condition must be blank or match:** customer, SKU, list currency,
   ports (a port group counts for its ports), container type, container class and
   Incoterm.
4. **The most specific match wins.** Each matching condition counts one point. A
   list linked to the customer's **market** counts ten, so it wins over any list
   without it. Markets don't exclude anyone: a list with markets still applies to
   other customers, just at lower rank.
5. **Ties go to the lower Priority number.**
6. **No match:** the item's **Default Price** is used if the line is per the item's
   default unit. Otherwise the amount is left as it is.

A typed amount is kept when the lookup comes back with the same list row it came
from. Opening a saved line never looks up a price.

## Things to know

- **Priority** is 1 to 100. New lists start at 5.
- **Expires stops lookups at the start of that day.** The grid still shows the list
  as in force on its Expires day. To use a list through a day, expire it the next
  day.
- A list's lines are saved one at a time, each in its own dialog.
- **Clone** copies the list, its markets and its lines to a new list. Give it a new
  description. The lines are copied when you first save.
- Coupon codes, ship dates and discounts can't be edited here, and are left as they
  are. A list with a coupon code is never chosen automatically.
- The grid starts on today's date, showing lists in force today. Clear the date to
  see them all.
