# Carrier Service Contract Compliance

Checks bookings against carrier service contracts: how many TEUs moved under each
contract, and which bookings could have but didn't use a contract rate.

## What's included

- **Contracted bookings:** bookings with a rate from a contract. An amended contract
  (one linked to an earlier one) counts towards the original.
- **Non-contracted bookings,** marked \*: bookings with no contract rate that sailed
  while one of the carrier's contracts was in force. You can leave these out.
- Only contracts in force on the chosen date, or whose bookings' contracts were.
- **Canceled bookings are left out.**
- **Only bookings with sea details.**
- Narrowed by shipper and carrier. A booking with no shipper appears only when the
  not-assigned choice is included.

## Things to know

- TEUs are the booking's container quantity × the TEUs of its container class (or
  the container type's class).
- Compare the contracted total with the contract's minimum TEUs.
