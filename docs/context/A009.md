# Quick Sale

A quick sale is a sales order, outbound cargo and scale ticket on one ShipperXL
Desktop screen, for selling straight from your yard to a customer who drives in.

## Things to know

- A quick sale is an ordinary sales order with one line, marked with the source
  *QuickSale*. It appears in the sales order lists like any other.
- The number comes from the sales order number scheme when you first save.
- Tick the cargo option to record the vehicle, its scale ticket and the inventory
  taken.
- **Create Invoice** is available once the cargo is included, and only until an
  invoice exists. It saves first, then opens the new invoice.
- You can also create a purchase contract for the product from a vendor on the same
  screen. That needs a booking (shipment) number.
