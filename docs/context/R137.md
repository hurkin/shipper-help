# Estimated On Dock Inventory

Shows product sitting at the port on a given day: containers already delivered to
the carrier whose vessel hadn't sailed yet, with their inventory cost and the work
order costs spent on them.

## What's included

- **Cargo on a booking, delivered to the carrier on or before the day, whose booking
  sails after it** (the booking's planned departure, or else the shipment's).
- **Only cargo that fulfills a sales order.** The processor shown is from that
  fulfillment (or its work zone).
- Only rows with a SKU.

## Work order cost summary

- Work orders with a rate on the same on-dock cargo, totalled by work zone, worker
  and item category.
- **Cargo already invoiced is left out:** cargo with an invoice line dated on or
  before the day.
- A weight-based work order with no quantity uses its scale ticket's net weight, or
  else the cargo's.
