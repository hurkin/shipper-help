# Sales by Country

Totals invoiced sales by destination country and item, with tons and amounts.

## What's included

- **Invoices only,** dated within the dates, and only their lines.
- One row per country, item, processor and currency.

## Things to know

- **The country** is the destination country of a booking linked to the invoice.
  Without one, it is the country of the invoice's ship-to address, then the ship-to
  party's phone country, then the buyer's phone country. Otherwise *Unspecified*.
- **The amount is worked out on the report:** price × the secondary quantity
  converted to the pricing unit, or price × quantity when there is no secondary
  quantity.
- Tons come from the secondary quantity, as short tons or metric tons by your
  database's weight unit.
- The processor comes from the fulfillment of the line's cargo, or else from the
  linked booking's cargo, scale or fulfillment.
