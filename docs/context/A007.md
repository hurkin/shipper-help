# Terms

Terms are the payment terms offered on agreements, bills and invoices, such as
Net 30 or Prepaid.

## Things to know

- **Days Due** sets the due date: a bill or invoice is due its date plus these days.
  Changing a bill's date moves its due date.
- **Short Code** has special meaning when it matches a bill type. Terms coded
  *BILL*, *INVOICE* or *CREDIT* become the default terms for every new bill,
  invoice or credit memo of that type, ahead of the party's own terms.
- If there's no such code, new bills and invoices take the party's terms, then your
  company's, then the active terms with the fewest days due.
- **Additional Terms** written here are copied into an agreement's Additional Terms
  when these terms are picked, but only if the agreement has none yet.
- **External Reference** is the matching name in your accounting system.
- Terms are deactivated, not deleted.
