# SKUs and Inventory Balance

A SKU (stock-keeping unit) is one identifiable lot of a product item, such as one
grower's stack. Inventory is always counted by SKU, in a location. One item can
have many SKUs.

## SKU things to know

- **SKU** must be unique. Leave it blank on a new SKU to take the next number from
  the SKU number scheme.
- **Barcode Number** is assigned when the SKU is first saved and can't be changed.
- **Raw Inventory Unit** is the unit the SKU is counted in, such as a 3x4 bale. A SKU
  has only one. **It locks once the SKU has inventory.** If a lot comes in two unit
  types, use two SKUs.
- **Supplier Location** lists only that supplier's locations, and clears if you
  change the supplier. **Add location** adds one owned by the supplier, if you can
  edit locations. Use **Map** to set the latitude and longitude. A latitude or
  longitude of 0 clears both.
- **SKU Location Description** describes where the lot is. Everywhere else, a SKU is
  described by its item's description.
- **Default Work Zone** is where the lot is likely to be before it is received.
- **Year** and **Cutting** describe hay lots, and show on some reports.
- **Capacity Factor** raises or lowers how much of the lot is used per container. It
  shows as the factor on [SKUs Available to Sell](R127.md).
- **Completed/Exhausted** marks a lot as used up. Completed and inactive SKUs are
  hidden from searches unless you ask for them.
- **SKU pickers** across Shipper have an ⓘ button that shows the chosen SKU's
  details. Where a new lot makes sense, such as an agreement line or an inspection,
  **New SKU** creates one without leaving the page.
- **Clone** opens a new SKU with these details, named "(copy)", with no barcode.
- **Delete** is unavailable once a SKU has inventory history, because the history
  would be deleted with it. A SKU on agreement or bill lines can't be deleted
  either. Mark it inactive instead.

## SKU tabs

- **Status:** totals for the SKU: purchased and sold on contracts and orders,
  fulfilled, remaining at the supplier, on hand, and available to sell.
- **Related:** the agreement lines that use this SKU, with assigned and fulfilled
  quantities.
- **Balance:** what the SKU holds in each location, with weights. Click a row for
  its ledger in that location.
- **Ledger:** every inventory transaction, newest first, with running raw,
  processed and weight balances, 250 rows at a time.
- **Bill of Materials:** the components of the SKU's item (and any this SKU alone
  uses). Tick **Selected?** and enter a **Quantity** for each component this SKU
  uses, then click **Save**. A ticked component with no quantity takes 1.
  **Selected items only** hides the rest, and is on when any are selected.
  **Not on the item** marks a component since removed from the item's bill of
  materials. With write access to purchase orders, **Purchasing** shows a
  **Suggested Purchase** and **Add Purchase Order**: choose the supplier and
  Shipper creates a purchase order with an unpriced line for each component to
  buy, or, when there are none, one line for this SKU at its item's default cost.
  Save the bill of materials first.
- **Inspections:** the inspections that include this SKU, newest first. **Add
  Inspection** starts a new one with this SKU on it. See [Inspections](I008.md).

## Inventory Balance

**Inventory > Balance** shows one row per SKU per location, with raw, processed and
weight totals for everything found. Search by SKU, or by location, and narrow by
work zone, commodity, item, year or cutting. Click a row to see that SKU's ledger in
that location.

## How the balance is worked out

- A transaction counts only when it has a date and a location.
- Balances add up transactions by date and time, so the order things are recorded
  in matters.
- Weights come from inbound scale tickets. A tag deducted by barcode uses that tag's
  weight. A deduction by SKU uses the SKU's average weight of everything received up
  to that time.
