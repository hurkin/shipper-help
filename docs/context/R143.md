# Shipment Status (Legal)

A wide booking status list, printed on legal-size paper: each booking's customers,
lines, processors, vessel, ports, due dates, invoice date and queues.

## What's included

- Bookings that aren't canceled (or only canceled ones, if you choose).
- With due dates chosen, bookings whose cargo due or documents due falls within
  them. With sail dates chosen, bookings whose planned departure (booking's or
  shipment's) falls within them.
- **Only bookings with a carrier among those chosen.** A booking with no carrier
  never appears.
- Narrowed by forwarder, place of receipt and place of delivery. Bookings with none
  appear only when the not-assigned choice is included.
- Narrowed by queue: bookings in the queues chosen, and, if the no-queue choice is
  included, bookings in none of them.
- Sales lines are narrowed by work zone and processor, with the same not-assigned
  rule. A booking with no sales lines appears only when both not-assigned choices
  are included.

## Things to know

- Fulfilled counts the booking's cargo only.
- The invoice date is the latest invoice linked to the booking.
- A user from another company is offered only the forwarders their company is, or
  deals with. Your own staff are offered every one. See
  [Reports for outside users](../instructions/user-accounts.md?id=reports-for-outside-users).
