# Terminals

Terminals are the terminals within a port, used for a booking's loading and
discharge.

## Things to know

- Each terminal belongs to one port, and its region is the port's region.
- The list is filtered by country (the United States to start with) and region.
- On a booking, the POL and POD terminal lists show only the chosen port's
  terminals.
- Terminals are deactivated, not deleted.
