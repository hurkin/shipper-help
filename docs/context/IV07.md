# Letters of Credit

A letter of credit (LC) is the bank's promise of payment for a sale. In Shipper it is
a bill-type record with the LC's own details: issuing, reimbursing and notify banks,
credit amount and tolerance, issue and expiry dates, latest shipment date, drafts,
description of goods and the beneficiary certificate wording. Letters of credit are
entered in ShipperXL Desktop.

## Things to know

- The number comes from the LC number scheme. Your company is the seller.
- **Linking an invoice:** an invoice can point at an LC only when both have the same
  buyer and seller.
- **Linking a booking:** link the LC to a booking from the booking's invoices
  section. Invoices then created from that booking are linked to the LC.
- **Documents and tasks:** LC documentation tasks list the documents the LC
  requires. Certificates such as the beneficiary certificate and bank sight draft
  are printed from **Reports**.
- Completing, posting and payments work as on invoices. Posting a payment starts any
  *Letter of Credit Payment Posted* scheduled jobs.
