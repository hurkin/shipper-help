# Letters of Credit

A letter of credit (LC) is the customer's bank's promise to pay for a sale. Like an
invoice, your company is the seller and the customer the buyer. Letters of credit
are under **Documentation**, below Credit Memos.

## Creating one

- **New Letter of Credit:** you choose the customer. The number comes from the LC
  number scheme, the date is today, and the LC starts in the LC starting queues.
- **Terms** for a new LC are, in order: terms whose short code is *LC*, the
  customer's terms, your company's terms, or else the active terms with the fewest
  days due.

## Tabs

- **General:** the customer, ship-to and your company with their printed
  addresses, the key dates, memo, references and currency.
- **Bank Parties:** the **Issuing**, **Advising** and **Reimbursing** banks. Picking
  a bank or one of its addresses rewrites its printed address, which you can edit
  afterwards. **Reference 1a** and **2a** are the bank's references.
- **Bank Details:** the credit amount and tolerance, advice and reference numbers,
  number of amendments, presentation period, issue, expiration and latest shipment
  dates, drawee, drafts at, the beneficiary certificate declaration, the
  description of goods and services, and additional notes.
- **Product & Terms:** product description, incoterm and additional terms.
- **Documentation:** the documents the LC requires (see below).
- **Payments**, **Notes**, **Related** and **Attachments** work as on
  [invoices](IV01.md). An LC has no line items.

## Things to know

- **The total is the credit amount.** Payments are taken off it, and the grid's
  Open and Paid views use it.
- **Linking a sales order or booking:** choose the LC on the sales order's
  **Terms** tab or on the booking's **Invoices** tab, then save. A record has one
  LC. Choosing another replaces it, and clearing the field removes it.
- **Invoices:** an invoice created from a booking with an LC points to that LC. You
  can also choose an invoice's LC on its **Product & Terms** tab. Only LCs with the
  invoice's buyer and seller are offered.
- **Related** lists the agreements and bookings tied to the LC. It is read-only.
  Links are made from the sales order or the booking, or by creating invoices.
- Completing, posting, payments and deleting follow the same rules as
  [invoices](IV01.md). Posting a payment starts any *Letter of Credit Payment
  Posted* scheduled jobs.

## Documentation

- The **Documentation** tab lists the documents the LC requires, such as the
  beneficiary certificate and bank sight draft. It takes the tasks of the
  customer, seller and ship-to when it opens, or when you save a change to them.
  Those can't be removed here. You can add more, and remove the ones you added.
- Bookings and agreements linked to the LC take its tasks.
- The tab needs the [Booking Documents](D001.md) permission. Task changes are saved
  with **Save**.
- Certificates such as the beneficiary certificate and bank sight draft are printed
  from **Reports**.
