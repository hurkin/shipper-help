# Unfulfilled Agreement Schedule

Lists open agreement lines by SKU in order of when they are needed, with how many
units are still needed and the work orders planned to bring them in.

## What's included

- Run for a SKU, an agreement, or a company, optionally for one commodity.
- **For an agreement or company, it works out the SKUs on those lines, then lists
  every open line for those SKUs** on any agreement, not just that company's.
- Lines that **aren't fully fulfilled** (or have no ordered quantity).
- Sales quotes, orders drawn from a contract, canceled and completed agreements, and
  completed lines are left out.
- A line assigned to several bookings appears once for each.

## Things to know

- **Units needed** is the quantity assigned to unfinished inbound work orders. With
  none, it is ordered minus fulfilled. Container quantities are converted to the
  SKU's raw unit.
- **Need date** is the earliest planned start of those work orders, or else the
  booking's planned departure.
- The party is the work order's worker when there is one, otherwise the customer
  (sales) or supplier (purchases).
