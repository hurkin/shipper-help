# Locations

A location is a place inventory can sit: a barn, a warehouse bay, a pad or yard, or a
specific bin in a zone, aisle and row.

## Things to know

- **Short Code** must be unique. It is what people type and what movement screens
  match on, along with the barcode and description.
- **The barcode is assigned for you** when the location is saved, and can't be
  typed in.
- **Work Zone** groups inventory reports and balances by area. Set it on every
  location.
- **Owner** is usually your company, but can be a leased space or a supplier's yard.
  A supplier's locations are the ones offered as a SKU's supplier location.
- **Raw**, **WIP**, **Finished**, **Cargo Location** and **3rd Party** describe what
  the location holds. A new location starts as Raw, WIP and Finished.
- Locations are deactivated, not deleted.
- This screen appears only for people who can edit locations. Everyone can still
  pick locations elsewhere.
