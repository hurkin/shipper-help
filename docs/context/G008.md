# Companies and Contacts

A party is anyone you deal with: customers, suppliers, carriers, processors,
workers and the people who work for them. A party is either a **company** or a
**contact** (a person). Contacts are linked to companies.

## Things to know

- **Categories** say what a company does for you: customer, supplier, carrier and
  so on. A company can have several. They are optional on a contact. The lists can
  be filtered by category.
- **Active**: untick it on a party you no longer deal with. An inactive party is
  left out of the pickers and of the lists' **Active** view (choose **Inactive** or
  **All** to find it), but every record already naming it still shows it.
- **Only a name is required.** Pickers throughout Shipper can add a new company or
  contact with just a name, and fill in the rest later.
- **Short Code** is an abbreviation used on reports where space is tight.
- **SCAC** is the carrier's standard code. Carriers need it for shipline watching.
  See this [list of carrier SCAC codes](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1EZcyvtZ4QWE0xUIMKNDwzu03WMiMwgmL2Fvh7Ays-Tw).
- **Native Name** (in ShipperXL Desktop) is the company's name in another language,
  which can be used on documents.
- **IATA codes** are exactly 2 and 3 characters.
- A company's **terms** become the default terms on new bills and invoices for it.
  Its **market** affects which price and cost lists apply first.
- **Tax ID** sits beside Default Terms and Market. Contacts have one too, as in
  ShipperXL Desktop.
- **Transactions Opt-In** and **Marketing Opt-In**, below the categories, are the
  party's opt-ins from ShipperXL Desktop's privacy settings. A new party is opted in
  to both.
- The ⓘ button in any party picker opens the party's details, addresses and
  contacts without leaving your page.

## Addresses

- Click an address to edit it.
- **Choose the country first.** A new address starts with the party's country, or
  else your company's. For a country that uses regions, **Region** is picked from
  its list. The phone and postal code take the country's format as you type.
- An address is marked **AR**, **AP** or **Delivery**. A new address starts with all
  three. Create Bills uses a supplier's only address, or else its first AR address.
- How an address prints depends on its country's address style.

## Contacts

- **Add Contact** links an existing person or creates a new one, and relates them to
  the company.
- A person's page lists their companies instead.
- On agreements, a party's contact list shows only that party's contacts.

## Documentation

The **Documentation** tab lists the documentation tasks this party always needs,
such as a phytosanitary certificate with two copies to the buyer. Sales orders,
contracts and quotes take them when the party is their customer, seller or
ship-to, and pass them on to their bookings. Save to keep changes. See
[Booking Documents](D001.md).

## Attachments

The **Attachments** tab holds files for the party, such as signed contracts or
certificates. Images show as thumbnails; other files open when clicked. Tick
files and click **Email Attachments** to email them. See
[Email Attachments](../instructions/email-attachments.md?id=email-attachments-from-a-record).

## My Signature

On your own contact (one of your company's contacts with your sign-in email), the
**General** tab ends with **My Signature**: your signature, printed on documents you
sign. Upload a PNG or JPEG of about 250 x 75 pixels. It is saved as soon as you
choose it, without the page's Save. **Delete** removes it. Nobody else sees this on
your contact.

To make one in Microsoft Paint, set the image size to 250 x 75 pixels, draw your
signature with the pencil, and save it as a PNG. To put signatures on agreements, see
[Agreement](R116.md).

## Your own company

Your own company can be changed only by an owner. Being one of its contacts makes a
person staff, and staff can see every record, so who is staff is an owner's to
decide:

- Only an owner relates a party to your company, or removes that relation.
- On a staff member's contact, only an owner changes **Email** or **Active**. The
  email is what they sign in with, and an inactive contact can't sign in as staff.
  Both show greyed out to everyone else, and converting the contact isn't offered.
- Staff who aren't owners can't change another staff member's contact at all. It
  opens read-only, marked *Staff member - owners only*. They can still change their
  own, except its Email and Active.
- Only an owner relates a party to another staff member.

## Converting

A company can be converted to a contact, or back. The party keeps its addresses,
relations, categories and every record it is on. Details only the old kind has
(such as a company's short code, SCAC and port terminals, or a person's title and
other phones) are deleted, and Shipper lists them before you confirm. Converting
needs delete rights.
