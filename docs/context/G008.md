# Companies and Contacts

A party is anyone you deal with: customers, suppliers, carriers, processors,
workers and the people who work for them. A party is either a **company** or a
**contact** (a person). Contacts are linked to companies.

## Things to know

- **Categories** say what a company does for you: customer, supplier, carrier and
  so on. A company can have several. They are optional on a contact. The lists can
  be filtered by category.
- **Only a name is required.** Pickers throughout Shipper can add a new company or
  contact with just a name, and fill in the rest later.
- **Short Code** is an abbreviation used on reports where space is tight.
- **SCAC** is the carrier's standard code. Carriers need it for shipline watching.
- **IATA codes** are exactly 2 and 3 characters.
- A company's **terms** become the default terms on new bills and invoices for it.
  Its **market** affects which price and cost lists apply first.
- The ⓘ button in any party picker opens the party's details, addresses and
  contacts without leaving your page.

## Addresses

- An address is marked **AR**, **AP** or **Delivery**. Create Bills uses a
  supplier's only address, or else its first AR address.
- How an address prints depends on its country's address style.

## Contacts

- **Add contact** links an existing person or creates a new one, and relates them to
  the company.
- A person's page lists their companies instead.
- On agreements, a party's contact list shows only that party's contacts.

## Documentation

The **Documentation** tab lists the documentation tasks this party always needs,
such as a phytosanitary certificate with two copies to the buyer. Sales orders,
contracts and quotes take them when the party is their customer, seller or
ship-to, and pass them on to their bookings. Save to keep changes. See
[Booking Documents](D001.md).

## Your own company

Your own company and its contacts can be changed only by an owner. Being one of its
contacts makes a person staff, and staff can see every record.

## Converting

A company can be converted to a contact, or back. The party keeps its addresses,
relations, categories and every record it is on. Details only the old kind has
(such as a company's short code, SCAC and port terminals, or a person's title and
other phones) are deleted, and Shipper lists them before you confirm. Converting
needs delete rights.
