# Vendor Receipts Statement

Lists what each supplier delivered over a period, load by load, with weights and
amounts owed. Use it to settle with growers and suppliers.

## What's included

Two kinds of rows:

1. **Purchase fulfillment:** purchase order and contract lines fulfilled by a cargo.
   The cargo must be **inbound, or neither inbound nor outbound** (processing), and
   loaded or unloaded within the dates (whole days in your time zone). The
   fulfilled quantity can't be 0. The supplier is the agreement's seller.
2. **Received with no agreement:** inventory added to an inbound (or processing)
   cargo in the dates, for a cargo with **no fulfillment at all**. The supplier is
   the SKU's supplier, and the amount is 0. These rows appear only when the cargo
   has a scale ticket on one of the scales chosen.

- Only inventory rows with a location and a date count.
- Purchase rows from cargo with no scale ticket are always included. Otherwise the
  ticket's scale must be one of those chosen.
- SKU filter: an exact SKU match.
- An item with no commodity appears only when the not-assigned choice is included.

## Things to know

- **Split loads:** when a cargo carries several SKUs, each line's weight is its
  share of the cargo's net weight, by units. These rows are starred.
- When the line is priced per a weight unit, the amount is price × that weight.
  Otherwise it is price × fulfilled quantity.
- A negative fulfilled quantity (a return) gives a negative weight and amount.
- **Moisture** is the average of the *MOISLO* and *MOISHI* custom fields on
  inspections of the cargo.
