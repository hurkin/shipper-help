# Items Available to Sell

Totals, by item, how much product is contracted, received, on hand, already sold
and still available to sell.

## What's included

- **SKUs that aren't completed,** of product items only. Choose active or inactive
  SKUs.
- An item with no commodity appears only when the not-assigned choice is included.
- You can hide items with nothing available.

## How available is worked out

> Available = purchased − sold on sales orders − sold on sales contracts

- **Purchased** is the quantity on purchase contracts, or what has been fulfilled from
  them if that is more.
- **Sales orders drawn from a sales contract aren't counted again;** only orders not
  drawn from a contract are subtracted, plus the sales contracts themselves.
- Sales in containers are converted to the SKU's raw units using its capacity factor.
- **Remaining at supplier** is contracted minus fulfilled, never below 0.
- On hand counts rows with a location.
