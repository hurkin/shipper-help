# Carrier Service Contracts

A carrier service contract holds the freight rates agreed with a carrier for a
period of time, usually by origin and destination port. Contracts are kept in
ShipperXL Desktop.

## Things to know

- A contract has a carrier, a contract number, effective and expiry dates, a
  currency and, if agreed, a minimum number of TEUs. Free-time terms can be noted
  for origin and destination.
- **Rates** are per container class (and optionally a container type), from an
  origin port to a destination port, with a base rate. A rate can also be set per
  freight class.
- **Fees** can apply to the whole contract or to one rate, as an amount or a
  percentage of the rate or of the booking.
- **A booking locks its rate.** Once a rate is chosen on a booking, it stays even if
  the contract is amended later. Update affected bookings one by one.
- **Clone** copies a contract with its rates and fees, and links the copy to the
  original. Use it for amendments and renewals. **Related** lists linked contracts.
