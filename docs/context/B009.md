# Carrier Service Contracts

A carrier service contract holds the freight rates agreed with a carrier for a
period of time, by origin and destination port. Open them in **Lists > Carrier
Contracts**.

## The list

- **Current** (the default) shows contracts that haven't expired yet, **Expired**
  the rest, and **All** both. Narrow it by carrier, or by **In force on** a day.
- Each row shows its status (**In force**, **Upcoming** or **Expired**), its master
  contract, and how many rates and amendments it has.

## General

- A contract has a contract number, a carrier, a currency, effective and expiry
  dates and, if agreed, a minimum number of TEUs.
- **Master Contract** is the contract this one amends. Pick one of the carrier's
  other contracts, or clear it to make this contract a master. A contract can't be
  its own master or have one of its own amendments as its master. The link under the
  field opens the master.
- **Origin Free Time** applies to every rate unless a rate has its own.
  **Destination Free Time** applies to a rate that has none of its own.
- **Memo** is highlighted when it has text.
- **Contract Fees** apply to every booking on the contract, per container or per
  booking, with a memo.

## Rates

Each row is one lane: origin, destination, container class (and type, if one),
freight classes and base rate. Each fee used on the contract's rates has its own
column, and **Total** is the base rate plus the per-container fees, as a booking's
estimate adds them. A fee charged per booking is marked **/bkg** and isn't in the
total.

- Type a base rate or a fee straight into its cell. Clear a fee's cell to take the
  fee off that rate.
- The pencil opens the whole rate: ports, container class and type, freight
  classes, fees, destination free time, memo and, if needed, an origin free time of
  its own.
- The copy icon starts a new rate from that one. Lanes often differ only in the
  destination.
- Tick rates to **Set Fee** on all of them at once, or to **Remove** them. Removing
  says how many bookings chose those rates. Those bookings keep their other details
  but lose the rate.
- A rate can't have the same fee twice. A fee with no amount isn't kept.

Nothing is saved until you click **Save**, which saves the contract, its fees and
the rates you changed together.

## Amendments

**New Amendment** copies the contract with its rates and fees, under the same
master, and suggests the next number (for example *LAX0184B26, 7*). Change the
dates, then the rates that changed. Notes, attachments, the minimum TEUs and the
memo aren't copied.

**Related** lists the master and all its amendments, and the bookings that chose
this contract's rates.

## Things to know

- **A booking locks its rate.** Once a rate is chosen on a booking, it stays even if
  the contract is amended later. Update affected bookings one by one: choose it
  under **Estimated Freight Rate** on the booking's **Freight** tab. See
  [Bookings](B008.md?id=freight-rate).
- A contract can't be deleted while a booking uses one of its rates, or while it has
  amendments.
