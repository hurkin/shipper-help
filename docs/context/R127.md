# SKUs Available to Sell

Shows how much of each SKU is still available to sell, with what is bought, sold,
received and on hand.

## What's included

- **Active SKUs that aren't completed,** of product items only.
- A SKU with no supplier, or an item with no commodity, appears only when the
  not-assigned choice is included.
- **Only SKUs with something available.** By default that is available to sell. The
  other option counts what is still at the supplier plus what is on hand.
- Figures are shown in the unit you choose. A SKU whose figures aren't in that unit
  doesn't appear.
- Keywords search the item description, or match an item number exactly.
- **Inspection requirements:** you can require a positive or negative result on a
  field of an inspection type. Only SKUs with such an inspection appear.
- Run from an inspection, it lists that inspection's SKUs.

## Things to know

- Hay analysis figures (moisture, protein, ADF, NDF, TDN, RFV, nitrate) come from
  the SKU's latest *Hay Analysis* inspection that is marked public.
- The quote column marks SKUs on open sales quotes: \* when a quote has no expiry,
  √ when one is current.
