# Booking Status

Lists bookings sailing in a period with their carrier, forwarder, customer, vessel,
ports, due dates and queues.

## What's included

- Bookings whose planned departure (booking's, or else shipment's) falls within the
  dates. A booking with no departure date is included when its cargo due or
  documents due falls within them.
- **Only ocean bookings with sea details.** A booking with no sea record doesn't
  appear.
- **Only bookings with a carrier among those chosen.**
- Narrowed by forwarder, shipper and customer. Bookings with none appear only when
  the not-assigned choice is included.
- Narrowed by queue: bookings in the queues chosen, and, if the no-queue choice is
  included, bookings in none of them.
- **Canceled bookings are included,** with their canceled date.

## Things to know

- The customer is the buyer of a sales order or contract line assigned to the
  booking. If several, one is shown. With none, the booking's shipper is shown.
- A user from another company is offered only the forwarders, shippers and
  customers their company is, or deals with. Your own staff are offered every one.
  See [Reports for outside users](../instructions/user-accounts.md?id=reports-for-outside-users).
