# Traceability

Shipper keeps a trail from each inbound load to each container shipped.

- **Every inbound load is recorded:** the grower, SKU, weight, unit count and
  scale pictures. Each purchase line carries a SKU, which usually stands for one
  grower, year, field or pivot.
- **Every block gets a tag.** Each tag has a unique barcode printed for the block.
  The tag follows the block through every move until it is used.
- **Pressed ahead of time:** once a block is double-compressed and blended with
  others, it loses its own identity but keeps its SKU. The SKU can be printed on
  the processed product.
- **Pressed straight into a container:** each block from the inbound loads can be
  traced to the container it was loaded into.
- **Who and when:** each container records when and by whom product was unloaded,
  loaded and moved.

To follow a SKU, open it in **Lists > SKUs** and look at its **Ledger** tab, or click
its row in **Inventory > Balance**. The ledger page you reach from Balance can also
show one tag's history: scan or type the tag there.
