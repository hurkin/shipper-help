# Hardware and Supplies

ShipperXL runs on the computers and phones you already have. Everything on this
page is optional: extra components that make gathering and capturing information
faster and more reliable, such as scanning tags instead of typing them, printing
labels, and recording weights and pictures at the scale without anyone at a desk.

## Desktop barcode printer

Prints inventory tags and SKU labels. To order, you need all three:

- [Zebra ZD411t barcode label printer](https://www.barcodesinc.com/zebra/part-zd4a022-t01e00ez.htm)
  ([setup](label-printer.md))
- [Zebra Z-Select 4000T 2.25" x 4" thermal transfer labels](https://www.barcodesinc.com/zebra/part-10009527.htm)
- [Zebra 800132-102 thermal transfer ribbon](https://www.barcodesinc.com/zebra/part-800132-102.htm)

![[hardware-label-printer.png|200]]

## Mobile scanner

[ShipperXL Mobile](install-mobile.md) needs no special hardware, but most people
find a device with a built-in long-range scanner far more accurate and reliable than
a phone camera.

- [Zebra TC27](https://www.barcodesinc.com/zebra/part-wcmta-t27b6abc2-na.htm): an
  all-in-one Android phone and barcode scanner, for faster scanning.

![[hardware-mobile-scanner.png|150]]

## Scale kiosk

Self-service at the scale for trusted drivers and employees. With cameras and a
readerboard it is a complete solution. To order and assemble your own:

- [Microsoft Surface Pro](https://amzn.to/3QtDEqq) ([setup](install-kiosk.md))
- A desktop barcode scanner (above)
- [CTA Digital anti-theft security grip and stand](https://amzn.to/4hUEEjw)
- [Serial cable adapter](https://amzn.to/3DigGzk), to connect to the scale head's
  serial cable. Your scale service can help with this.
- [Micro USB adapter](https://amzn.to/41kgRCy)
- A letter-size laser printer of your choice

![[hardware-kiosk.png|400]]

## Scale cameras

Take pictures from up to two cameras per scale. Every scale ticket records the
weight and the pictures. Mount them on poles at each end of the scale, or wherever
they get a good view. Each needs a 110V outlet and WiFi.

- [TP-Link C325WB outdoor camera](https://amzn.to/4ig32Mh)

![[hardware-scale-camera.png|200]]

## Readerboard

A sign for driver-on-scale operation: drivers use ShipperXL Mobile to weigh in,
weigh out and be emailed a weight ticket.

- To order, contact sales@shipperxl.com. $650 each.
- Mount up to two per scale, so drivers can see one coming in from either direction.
- The only wire is 110V power. It needs WiFi to work with the
  [kiosk](kiosk.md).
