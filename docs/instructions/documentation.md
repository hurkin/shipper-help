# Prepare Documentation

Documentation means phytosanitary certificates, bills of lading and any other
certificates that go with a booking.

## Set up what each customer needs

1. Set up the master list of tasks in **Admin > Documentation Tasks**. Each task can
   name a document type, a Shipper report, or a report from another system.
2. Open the customer and go to its **Documentation** tab. Add the tasks it always
   needs, with the copies for each party and who gets the originals. Save.

Sales orders, contracts and quotes for that customer take these tasks when they
open. Add anything extra on the order's own **Documentation** tab. Bookings take
the tasks of the orders whose lines are on them.

## Prepare a booking's documents

1. Go to **Documentation > Booking Documents** and click the booking. It opens on
   its **Tasks** tab, showing what's needed.
2. Go to **Documents** and click **Add Document**. Choose the document type.
3. The document number fills in with the booking number.
4. Click **Fill Fields**. Shipper gathers what it can from the booking and fills
   the fields.
5. Complete anything left blank and click **Save**.
6. Print the document from **Reports**. Close the report to come back to the
   document.
7. Back on **Tasks**, tick **Complete** for each task that's done, and save the
   booking.

## Good to know

- What **Fill Fields** fills is decided by the document type's setup. See
  [Document Types](../context/D002.md).
- Tasks that came from the customer or the order can't be removed from the booking.
  Change them on the customer or the order.
- Once a document exists for an invoice or a booking, that invoice or booking can't
  be deleted.

See also: [Booking Documents](../context/D001.md), [Documentation Tasks](../context/S026.md).
