# Enter a Claim

Record a customer claim against a container as a credit memo.

1. Go to **Documentation > Credit Memos** and click **New**.
2. Choose the customer. Your company is the seller.
3. Add one line per container claimed:
   - **Quantity:** 1
   - **Amount:** the claim amount for that container
   - **Per:** Container
   - **Reference 1:** the container number
4. Put the reason in the credit memo's **Memo**, for example "Dirty color, bad
   smell". An item or description on the line is optional.
5. Save.

See also: [Credit Memos](../context/IV05.md).
