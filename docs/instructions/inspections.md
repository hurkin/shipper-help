# Record Customer Visits and Other Inspections

An inspection records who looked at which SKUs or containers, when, and what they
found. Inspections are entered in ShipperXL Desktop.

1. Open **Inspections** and click **Add New**.
2. Choose the **Inspection Type**, for example *Customer*. The type decides which
   extra fields appear.
3. In **Inspected By**, choose the customer.
4. Add the SKUs (or containers) inspected.
5. Enter the date and time of the inspection.
6. Add details and attachments as needed, and save.

Your name appears at the bottom of the printed inspection form as the person who
created or last edited it.

From an inspection you can select SKUs and click **Create Quote** to start a sales
quote for them.

See also: [Inspections](../context/I008.md), [Inspection Types](../context/I007.md).
