# Record Customer Visits and Other Inspections

An inspection records who looked at which SKUs or cargo, when, and what they found.

1. Open **Lists > Inspections** and click **New**. To inspect a particular cargo or
   SKU, open it instead, go to its **Inspections** tab and click
   **Add Inspection**: it is added for you.
2. Choose the **Inspection Type**, for example *Customer*. The type decides which
   fields appear below it, and can't be changed after saving.
3. In **Inspected By**, choose the customer or inspector.
4. Check the **Inspection Date**. It starts at now.
5. Fill in the type's fields.
6. On **Related Items**, add the SKUs or cargo inspected, with a comment on each if
   needed.
7. Save. Then add photos or other files on **Attachments**.

Your name appears at the bottom of the printed inspection form as the person who
created or last edited it.

## After a customer visit

- **Directions** opens a route to every SKU on the inspection.
- To quote what the customer saw, tick SKUs on **Related Items** and click
  **Create Quote**. A sales quote to the customer opens with a line for each SKU,
  ready for quantities and prices.
