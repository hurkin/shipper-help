# Billing

How ShipperXL bills your subscription, and how to pay.

## How billing works

- ShipperXL bills **monthly, after the fact**: you are invoiced after the month in
  which the usage happened.
- **Charges follow usage,** so the amount changes from month to month. Your invoice
  is worked out from active users, features used, documents generated, support
  provided and other activity recorded in ShipperXL during the month, plus a fixed
  platform fee for some editions.
- Invoices are created automatically from your ShipperXL usage. The usage counts
  behind every line are shown on the invoice.

## Ways to pay

- **Bank debit** is preferred, with no fees:
  - **US:** ACH.
  - **Canada:** Pre-Authorized Debit (PAD / ACSS), in Canadian dollars at last
    year's average exchange rate against our USD pricing.
- **Major cards** are accepted. As card fees rise, card payments may carry a
  surcharge in future.
- **Manual pay** or **autopay**.
- **Prepayment** by bank debit, with a modest discount applied as a credit. Your
  monthly invoices then draw down the credit balance.

## Autopay

Autopay charges each invoice automatically, so nothing needs paying by hand.

- **Enrolling:** you enroll once, through a secure link we send you. You enter your
  bank details on the payment processor's page. ShipperXL never sees or stores your
  account or routing numbers. Enrolling authorizes recurring debits: an ACH
  authorization for US accounts, or a Pre-Authorized Debit agreement for Canadian
  accounts.
- **After enrolling:** every invoice is debited automatically, whatever the amount.
  The invoice is still emailed to you before the debit runs, so you always know the
  amount in advance.
- **Turning it off or changing banks:** email billing@shipperxl.com for a link, or use
  the link sent with your invoices. Changing bank accounts needs a new
  authorization. The old one can't be moved to a different account.

## Payment timeline

You are responsible for providing funds by the invoice's due date. Each method takes
a different time:

- **Cards** are charged immediately, and we record the payment within an hour.
  Autopay by card is charged on the due date.
- **Bank debit** takes 2 to 5 business days to settle, so start it in time to settle
  by the due date. Autopay by bank debit starts 3 business days before the due date.
  We allow 3 more business days for it to settle, and the invoice counts as paid on
  time.

## Failed and returned payments

A debit can fail for insufficient funds, a closed or changed account, or a bank
block on debits. Banks can also return a debit after it seemed to settle. If a
payment fails or is returned, the invoice reopens and we contact your billing
contact.

## Past due accounts

Invoices more than 10 calendar days past due may lead to one or more of:

- reminders and notices
- suspension of access to the system
- late fees
- reactivation fees

Suspension doesn't affect your data. It is kept safe and available for at least 30
days.

## Changes to your account

To change your billing address, billing email or anything else about your account,
email billing@hurkin.com.

## Taxes

No taxes are collected on our services at this time. Sales tax may be required in
future for some customers in Washington State. You will be told in advance if taxes
will be charged.

## Common questions

**Why is my invoice a different amount every month?** Charges follow your actual
usage. Shipping more in a month raises the invoice, and shipping less lowers it. The
usage counts behind every line are shown on the invoice.

**Can I pay from a Canadian bank account?** Yes. Canadian customers pay by
Pre-Authorized Debit from a Canadian bank account. It works the same as ACH for US
customers: the same enrollment and the same automatic collection.

**Is autopay limited to a maximum invoice amount?** No. Autopay charges the full
invoice, whatever its size.

**My invoice says paid, but the money hasn't left my account yet. Why?** Settlement
takes 2 to 5 business days after we start the debit. The invoice closes when the
funds settle. Your bank may show the payment as pending until then.

**How do I dispute a charge?** Email billing@hurkin.com before the due date, with the
invoice number and the line in question.
