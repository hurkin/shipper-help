# Reserve Inventory with a Sales Contract

Put a SKU on a sales contract line to set it aside for a customer before any order
or booking exists.

1. Check that the customer exists in **Lists > Companies**. If not, add the
   company and tick **Customer** under its categories.
2. Add the customer's contacts on the company's **Contacts** tab.
3. Go to **Outbound > Sales Contracts**, click **New** and choose the customer.
4. Add one line per SKU:
   - Choose the **Inventory SKU**. Each line takes one SKU.
   - Choose the **Item**: the product you are selling.
   - Set **Quantity Ordered** in containers: Unit Type **Container**, and the
     container type (for example, a 40 ft container).
   - Enter the price in **Sales Amount** and what it is per.
5. Save, and print the **Agreement** report if the customer needs it.

Orders drawn from this contract count towards it, so the contract shows how much
of the reservation has shipped.

See also: [Sales Contracts](../context/A005.md).
