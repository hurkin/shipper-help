# Version 2026.4
**Deployment:**  October 31, 2026, 17:00–17:30 PDT

🟠 **Changes and** 🟢 **New Items**

1. 🟠 ShipperXL Desktop
    - Your windows app will prompt you to update when you restart the app. 
    - The update contains minimal changes, updated third-party components, and a new logo.
    - Also, the majority of Configuration windows and some key features are replaced with pages from the web app (below).

1. 🟠 ShipperXL Kiosk
    - Your kiosk app will prompt you to update when you restart the app. 
    - There were minor changes required to let the web app show the scale's live weight and camera image.
    - **Camera pictures:** with two cameras, the kiosk screen now always shows the pictures one above the other. The picture saved on the scale ticket, and the live picture in the web app, still follow the scale's **Stack images horizontally** setting in Admin > Scales, and are made from the same frames the operator saw when pressing RECORD.
    - Saved pictures are smaller files, and a single camera's picture is no longer stretched.
    - The live weight in the web app keeps updating even when a readerboard sign is off or unreachable, and drops to zero when the truck leaves the scale.

1. 🟢 Reference Data Updates
    - All databases now get quarterly updates to their countries, regions, sea ports, and SCAC carrier codes. 
    - This will appear sometime in November once we've confirmed a stable release. 
    - Those tables allow for items to be activated or inactivated, but not otherwise changed.

1. 🟢 Booking Sync Updates
    - Our booking integration primarily updates the POL and POD information, as well as container status. Prior to this release, the booking sync was also erroneously changing IPI and DLV information.
    - We are also piloting pulling vessel schedule, port cut-off, and other information from various US west coast ports, terminals, and shipline websites for weekly updates.
    - As always, any changes made by BookingSync or ScheduleSync will appear in your Booking Change queue

1. 🟢 ShipperXL Web
    - The ShipperXL web app is now in [beta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_release_life_cycle)! Sign in at [https://app.shipperxl.com](https://app.shipperxl.com). It does most of what ShipperXL Desktop does, and it works on your live database, so you can work in either one. Some things work differently on purpose (below), so please double-check your work as you get used to it, and send us your feedback.

    - 🟢 New in the web app
      - **Maps:** inventory balance by yard location, and SKUs with field coordinates.
      - **Inventory screens matching the mobile app:** Receive, Return, Move, Process, Ship Inventory.
      - **Auto Work Orders on the agreement:** create work order templates for the whole agreement, instead of copying line by line, though that's still available too.
      - **Queue diagram:** Admin > Queues draws each queue's path with related scheduled jobs.
      - **[Bulk Edit Work Orders](../context/W004.md?id=bulk-edit):** on the Work Order
        Scheduler, select work orders and click **Edit N Selected** to set the worker,
        work zone and center, work item type, cost, references, memo and more on all of
        them at once. Each work order's result is listed, and any that didn't save stay
        selected to try again.
      - **[Search everything](../instructions/getting-started.md?id=getting-around-the-web-app):**
        the search in the header is the web app's F3. Type 3 characters of a number,
        name or reference to find bookings, cargo, scale tickets, agreements, bills,
        parties, items, SKUs, work orders, and carrier contracts, and choose one to
        open it.
      - **[Record history](../instructions/record-history.md):** who changed each record
        and its lines, field by field.
      - **Recent:** the menu lists the last 15 records you opened, synced across your
        devices, and the browser tab names the open record.
      - **Help on every screen:** the help icon opens that screen's help page beside it.

    - 🟢 Easier than in ShipperXL Desktop
      - **[Bulk Edit Cargo](../context/B003.md?id=bulk-edit)** replaces the Fumigation
        Tool. Click **Bulk Edit** on the Inbound, Outbound or Process Cargo list, tick
        the cargo, and set the fumigated, opened, tested and ready-to-ship dates,
        container type, movement type, location, carrier status or memo on all of
        them at once. Each cargo's result is listed, and any that didn't save stay
        ticked to try again.
      - **[Bulk Edit Bookings](../context/S028.md):** click **Bulk Edit** on the Bookings
        or Booking Documents list and tick bookings straight from the list. Ticks are kept
        as you change views and searches, the edit dialog is laid out like a booking's
        General tab, and it also changes locations, terminal, equipment and planned
        delivery and adds a note. Each booking's result is listed, and any that didn't
        save stay ticked to try again.
      - **[Carrier Contracts](../context/B009.md):** under Lists. A contract's rates and
        fees are one grid, with a column per fee, a total per rate, and prices typed
        straight in. Set a fee on many rates at once, copy a rate to a new lane, and make
        an amendment with **New Amendment**.
      - **Bookings:** the General tab lays out the voyage as its four legs (IPI, POL, POD,
        DLV), with the days between each.

    - 🟠 Works differently from ShipperXL Desktop
      - **Searching:** each list has one search box and a few views or date ranges, in
        place of Desktop's long search forms. Some searches, such as bookings by shipper or
        port, or cargo by seal or chassis, aren't available.
      - **Agreement and bill numbers** can't be changed once the record is created. Your own company is always the buyer or seller on your side.
      - **Notes are always public.** The Public flag isn't offered.
      - **Agreement Copy To** is **Clone**.
      - **[Emailing attachments](../instructions/email-attachments.md?id=email-attachments-from-a-record):**
        tick attachments on any Attachments tab and click **Email Attachments**. Shipper
        sends the email from your database's mailbox, with replies and a copy going to
        you, and you can pick recipients from your contacts. The old way still works:
        **Download .eml** opens the email in Outlook as a draft, ready to send from your
        own mailbox, as Desktop's **Send** did.
      - **Posted records:** only an owner can post or unpost an agreement. On a posted
        bill, an owner can change only the Posted date.
      - **Deleting an agreement** is refused once any of its lines has fulfillments, and
        a line with fulfillments can't be deleted. See
        [Sales Orders](../context/A004.md?id=completed-posted-and-delete).
      - **Your own company and its contacts** can only be changed by an owner.
      - **A SKU with inventory history can't be deleted.** Deleting it would delete its
        ledger too, which ShipperXL Desktop does without warning.
      - **Payments:** anyone who can edit a bill can add a payment and set its Posted date.
        Once it is posted, only an owner can change, unpost or delete it, and a payment can
        be deleted only from an unposted bill.
      - **Outside users** (customers, suppliers and partners who sign in) see only the
        records their company is part of. Record-by-record access set in Desktop isn't
        used.

    - 🟠 Still only in ShipperXL Desktop
      - Quick Sales
      - Adding or removing a bill's Applies To links.
      - Adding notes to an agreement line (existing line notes are shown).
      - A memo on Move Inventory, partial-barcode lookup, and Recalculate Inventory on a
        SKU.
