# Version 2026.1

**Deployment:** December 31, 2025, 17:00–18:00 PST

Your database was read-only during the deployment. ShipperXL Desktop and ShipperXL
Kiosk ask you to update when you restart them or first sign in.

## Watch Shipline for changes

Shipper can now stay in touch with the shipline's systems and tell you about changes
to the bookings you choose. Changes to vessel, voyage and dates put the booking in a
queue, so you know which bookings changed. When you open the booking, you are asked
to accept the changes. The introductory price is $3.25 per booking.

To turn it on, tick **Watch Shipline for changes** on the booking. Carriers need a
SCAC code, a new field on the party screen.

(Since version 2026.2, changes are applied as soon as they arrive. See
[2026.2](2026.2.md).)

## New

- Queues show how many items they hold, with an alert symbol when items are overdue.
- The party screen links to HAAL to create or manage users.
- Items now have a bill of materials, a list of their SKUs, and a **Create SKU**
  button.
- SKUs now have a bill of materials, and a **Create Purchase Order** button on the
  On Hand tab.
- Sales order lines have a **Create Work Order** button.
- Parties can have signature, specification and logo images attached. They are used
  on the Agreement report.
- The in-product browser is back, so home and help pages show inside the app.
- The Work Order report shows specification images for the items and SKUs on it.
- When a report takes an "As Of" date, the date is shown in the report footer.

## Fixed

- Queues showed the wrong date and time in Pacific time, and counted items as overdue
  too early.
- Items didn't hide some fields that apply only to products.
- Work order search was slow with no search terms.
- The shipment screen took 1–2 seconds longer to open than it should.
